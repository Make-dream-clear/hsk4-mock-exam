两句俗语，两个表达重点

“熟能生巧”强调通过反复练习，逐渐熟练、掌握技巧。“万事开头难”强调刚开始做一件事时往往会遇到困难，常用来鼓励刚入门的人。判断时要看材料的重点：是在讲练习之后的进步，还是在讲起步时的困难？有的真实经历可能涉及两者；下面的练习要求选择最贴合题目重点的表达。

查看英文帮助 / English support

熟能生巧 (shú néng shēng qiǎo) emphasizes gaining skill through repeated practice. 万事开头难 (wàn shì kāi tóu nán) emphasizes the difficulty of starting and can encourage a beginner. A real experience may involve both. In these exercises, choose the expression that best matches the stated focus.

先判断情境 / Choose in context

选择后查看理由，也可以直接展开解析学习。Read the explanation after choosing, or open it to study first.

1. 小林每天练习打字。几个月后，他很少看键盘，速度也快了。这段话重点是练习后的进步，哪句最贴切？

Xiaolin practises typing daily. Months later he types faster without looking at the keyboard. Which saying best expresses this improvement through practice?

参考判断与逐项解释 / Compare the options

本题最贴切的选项 / Best fit: 熟能生巧

  • 熟能生巧：“每天练习”和后来速度变快，说明反复练习带来了技巧。Daily practice leads to greater skill.
  • 万事开头难：这句侧重刚开始的困难，但材料重点是几个月练习后的进步。This focuses on difficulty at the start, not the improvement described.

2. 安娜第一次学骑自行车，刚开始总是保持不了平衡。朋友想安慰她：“刚开始觉得难很正常。”哪句最贴合这句话？

Anna struggles to balance on her first day learning to cycle. Her friend wants to say that finding it hard at the beginning is normal. Which fits that message?

参考判断与逐项解释 / Compare the options

本题最贴切的选项 / Best fit: 万事开头难

  • 熟能生巧：可以用它鼓励今后多练，但题目要表达的是“刚开始觉得难很正常”。It could encourage future practice, but that is not the stated focus.
  • 万事开头难：“第一次”和“刚开始”表明重点是入门时的困难，符合朋友想表达的安慰。The clues point to the difficulty of starting.

3. 小周没有练过这类题，今天随便猜了一次，碰巧答对了。只凭这件事，能用哪句解释他答对的原因？

Xiaozhou has never practised these questions and happens to guess correctly once. Which saying explains why he got it right, based only on this information?

参考判断与逐项解释 / Compare the options

本题最贴切的选项 / Best fit: 这两句都不能解释

  • 熟能生巧：材料没有反复练习或技巧提高的证据，一次猜对不能说明熟能生巧。A lucky guess is not evidence of practice building skill.
  • 万事开头难：材料没有描述起步的困难，这句也不能解释为什么猜对。No starting difficulty is described, and this does not explain the lucky guess.
  • 这两句都不能解释：“碰巧”说明这次是偶然答对；不能把成功都归因于练习或起步困难。The information describes chance, not either saying.

卡住了？查看表达提示 / Need a hint?

“我选……，因为材料里说……。这说明……。另一句更强调……。比如……” 不必把俗语逐字翻译；说明它在这件事里表达的意思。

查看一种参考表达 / One possible answer

我选第一道题。小林每天练打字，现在很少看键盘，速度也快了，所以“熟能生巧”最贴切。这句话强调反复练习让人掌握技巧。“万事开头难”更强调刚开始时的困难，而这道题重点是练习后的进步。比如一个人每天练习切菜，后来切得更快、更均匀，也可以用“熟能生巧”。

表达可以不同；重点是意思清楚、信息准确。Other wording is welcome. Keep the meaning clear and accurate.

Scenario Dialogue / 情景对话

A student asks about a common Chinese saying. Read it aloud twice: once for meaning, once for fluency — the syllabus tests this task across listening and speaking.

A
老师常说"熟能生巧"，这是什么意思？
Lǎoshī cháng shuō "shú néng shēng qiǎo", zhè shì shénme yìsi?
The teacher often says "shú néng shēng qiǎo" — what does it mean?
B
意思是通过反复练习，逐渐熟练、掌握技巧，跟英语的 practice makes perfect 意思相近。
Yìsi shì tōngguò fǎnfù liànxí, zhújiàn shúliàn, zhǎngwò jìqiǎo, gēn Yīngyǔ de practice makes perfect yìsi xiāngjìn.
It means gaining skill through repeated practice, similar to the English saying practice makes perfect.
A
原来如此！那"万事开头难"呢？
Yuánlái rúcǐ! Nà "wànshì kāitóu nán" ne?
I see! And what about "wànshì kāitóu nán"?
B
这句话强调刚开始做一件事时往往会遇到困难，常用来鼓励刚入门的人。
Zhè jù huà qiángdiào gāng kāishǐ zuò yí jiàn shì shí wǎngwǎng huì yùdào kùnnan, cháng yòng lái gǔlì gāng rùmén de rén.
It emphasizes that starting something is often difficult, and is commonly used to encourage beginners.
A
汉语的俗语真有意思，一句话就能讲清楚一个道理。
Hànyǔ de súyǔ zhēn yǒu yìsi, yí jù huà jiù néng jiǎng qīngchu yí gè dàolǐ.
Chinese sayings are fascinating — one sentence can capture a whole truth.

Why HSK 4 Candidates Struggle with Proverbs & Sayings

Understand and roughly explain common Chinese sayings and famous quotes. The syllabus tests this receptively — recognizing what a saying means when you hear it in conversation or meet it in a short reading passage.

After studying the 21 selected words, practise combining them in extended contexts about proverbs & sayings. Use the dialogue to identify a sequence of events, opinions, or reasons, then describe them in your own words.

Key vocabulary to anchor your understanding: 表示 (biǎoshì, to express), 词语 (cíyǔ, word), 翻译 (fānyì, to translate), 解释 (jiěshì, to explain). Practise these words in listening dialogues (听力) and reading passages (阅读), using grammar patterns like fixed-patterns and rhetorical.

Proverbs & Sayings FAQ / 常见问题

How many HSK 4 words cover proverbs & sayings?

This page groups 21 words using this site’s topic tags for proverbs & sayings. It is a study selection, not an official separate topic word list or a guarantee of comprehension.

Which HSK 4 grammar points are most relevant to proverbs & sayings?

fixed-patterns and rhetorical are suggested here for practising proverbs & sayings contexts. The HSK 4 syllabus expects you to understand these patterns when they involve proverbs & sayings vocabulary.

What's the difference between HSK 3 and HSK 4 expectations on proverbs & sayings?

HSK 3 expects simple statements about proverbs & sayings (e.g., basic facts and short descriptions). HSK 4 raises the bar to "有一定复杂度" — handling extended contexts with opinions, comparisons, and reasoning. You'll need to express feelings (感受) and views (看法) about proverbs & sayings, not just describe them.
