HSK 4 介绍饮食情况 — Food & Dining

Describe food flavors, restaurant experiences, and cooking processes. Goes beyond ordering food (HSK 3) to discussing taste, food culture, and sharing dining experiences.

50
Words
4

Official Syllabus Requirement / 大纲要求

能听懂关于食物饮品、就餐情况、菜品制作情况等有一定复杂度的问题或介绍。如饮食味道、种类、特点、餐厅环境、服务、制作过程等。

读懂 → 开口 → 自查 → 再练 / Read · Speak · Review · Retry

完成一次点餐 / Make a restaurant order

根据菜单和同伴的口味完成点餐；答案可以不同，但要说明需求和理由。Order from the menu for your group and explain your choices.

自编学习活动，不是官方试题；用自己的话表达即可。Original learning activity, not an official test. No microphone needed.

你的任务：和朋友一起吃午饭

这是自编练习菜单：宫保鸡丁（辣，38元）、清蒸鱼（不辣，48元）、炒青菜（不辣，18元）、西红柿鸡蛋汤（不辣，16元），米饭每碗3元。你和一位朋友吃午饭。朋友不吃辣，你们想点一道荤菜、一道青菜、一份汤和两碗米饭。请向服务员说明需求，点菜，并询问大约需要等多久。价格仅用于本练习。

查看英文帮助 / English support

Practice menu: spicy kung pao chicken ¥38; non-spicy steamed fish ¥48; greens ¥18; tomato and egg soup ¥16; rice ¥3 per bowl. You and a friend want one meat or fish dish, greens, soup and two bowls of rice. Your friend does not eat spicy food. Explain your needs, place the order and ask how long it will take. These are fictional practice prices.

用自己的话说 / Your turn

点餐时可以查看菜单，无须背价格。Keep the menu open if useful.

0:00

按自己的节奏说，计时不会自动结束练习。No time limit; finish at your own pace.

  1. 说明人数和口味 / Say how many people and describe your preference
  2. 点齐菜、汤和米饭 / Order the dishes, soup and rice
  3. 询问等待时间，确认订单 / Ask about waiting time and confirm
卡住了？查看表达提示 / Need a hint?

“我们两个人，我朋友不吃辣。” → “请来……，再来……。” → “大约要等多久？” → 服务员确认：“一份清蒸鱼、一份青菜、一份汤、两碗米饭，对吗？”你怎样回应？菜单可以随时查看，不需要背价格。

查看一种参考表达 / One possible answer

我们两个人，我朋友不吃辣。我们点清蒸鱼，因为菜单上写着不辣。请再来一份炒青菜、一份西红柿鸡蛋汤和两碗米饭。大约要等多久？——服务员：一份清蒸鱼、一份青菜、一份汤、两碗米饭，对吗？——对，谢谢。

表达可以不同；重点是意思清楚、信息准确。Other wording is welcome. Keep the meaning clear and accurate.

自查要点 / Review points

  • 说明两人用餐和不吃辣 / State group size and preference
  • 选择符合口味的菜并说明理由 / Choose a suitable dish and explain
  • 说清菜、汤、米饭的数量 / Specify items and quantities
  • 询问等待时间并回应确认 / Ask and confirm

Scenario Dialogue / 情景对话

Ordering at a restaurant and asking about the dishes. Read it aloud twice: once for meaning, once for fluency — the syllabus tests this task across listening and speaking.

A
服务员，你们店有什么特色菜？
Fúwùyuán, nǐmen diàn yǒu shénme tèsè cài?
Waiter, what are your restaurant's special dishes?
B
我们的烤鸭最有名，皮脆肉嫩，很多客人专门为它来的。
Wǒmen de kǎoyā zuì yǒumíng, pí cuì ròu nèn, hěn duō kèrén zhuānmén wèi tā lái de.
Our roast duck is the most famous — crispy skin, tender meat. Many guests come especially for it.
A
好，来半只烤鸭。这个汤辣不辣？我不太能吃辣。
Hǎo, lái bàn zhī kǎoyā. Zhège tāng là bu là? Wǒ bú tài néng chī là.
Great, half a roast duck please. Is this soup spicy? I can't really eat spicy food.
B
这个是西红柿鸡蛋汤，一点儿都不辣，味道很清淡。
Zhège shì xīhóngshì jīdàn tāng, yìdiǎnr dōu bú là, wèidao hěn qīngdàn.
That is tomato and egg soup — not spicy at all, very light.
A
那再来一份这个汤。对了，能开发票吗？
Nà zài lái yí fèn zhège tāng. Duì le, néng kāi fāpiào ma?
Then one of those soups too. Oh — can you issue a receipt?
B
可以，吃完后凭小票到前台开就行。
Kěyǐ, chī wán hòu píng xiǎopiào dào qiántái kāi jiù xíng.
Yes — after your meal just bring the slip to the front desk.

Why HSK 4 Candidates Struggle with Food & Dining

Describe food flavors, restaurant experiences, and cooking processes. Goes beyond ordering food (HSK 3) to discussing taste, food culture, and sharing dining experiences.

After studying the 50 selected words, practise combining them in extended contexts about food & dining. Use the dialogue to identify a sequence of events, opinions, or reasons, then describe them in your own words.

Key vocabulary to anchor your understanding: 白酒 (báijiǔ, Chinese white liquor), 饼干 (bǐnggān, biscuit), 餐厅 (cāntīng, restaurant), 茶叶 (cháyè, tea leaves). Practise these words in listening dialogues (听力) and reading passages (阅读), using grammar patterns like complement and ba-sentence.

Food & Dining FAQ / 常见问题

How many HSK 4 words cover food & dining?

This page groups 50 words using this site’s topic tags for food & dining. It is a study selection, not an official separate topic word list or a guarantee of comprehension.

Which HSK 4 grammar points are most relevant to food & dining?

complement and ba-sentence are suggested here for practising food & dining contexts. The HSK 4 syllabus expects you to not only understand but also produce these patterns when they involve food & dining vocabulary.

What's the difference between HSK 3 and HSK 4 expectations on food & dining?

HSK 3 expects simple statements about food & dining (e.g., basic facts and short descriptions). HSK 4 raises the bar to "有一定复杂度" — handling extended contexts with opinions, comparisons, and reasoning. You'll need to express feelings (感受) and views (看法) about food & dining, not just describe them.
