饮食文化复述练习 / Retell a meal with friends

用自己的话讲清这次聚餐的经过和原因，不必逐句背诵。Retell what happened and why, in your own words.

自编学习活动，不是官方试题；用自己的话表达即可。Original learning activity, not an official test. No microphone needed.

第一次和朋友吃中餐

上周末，小林请外国朋友安娜到家里吃饭。安娜以前不太会用筷子，小林先给她做了示范。桌上有米饭、鱼、蔬菜和汤，大家一边吃饭，一边聊天。安娜发现，这次聚餐不是每个人只吃自己面前的菜，而是一起分享桌上的菜。她觉得这样很热闹，也有机会尝到不同的味道。饭后，安娜想把剩下的菜倒掉。小林建议把吃不完的菜妥善保存，避免浪费。安娜点了点头，说下次要请小林尝尝自己家乡的菜。

查看英文帮助 / English support

Last weekend, Xiaolin invited Anna to his home for a meal. She was not confident using chopsticks, so he demonstrated. They shared rice, fish, vegetables and soup while chatting. Anna enjoyed the lively meal and trying different flavors. Afterwards, she was about to throw away the leftovers. Xiaolin suggested storing them properly to avoid waste. Anna agreed and offered to introduce food from her hometown next time.

用自己的话说 / Your turn

原文已收起。卡住时可以看提示或回看原文。The passage is hidden; help is available whenever you need it.

0:00

按自己的节奏说，计时不会自动结束练习。No time limit; finish at your own pace.

  1. 谁邀请了谁？遇到什么困难？ / Who came, and what was difficult?
  2. 吃饭时怎样分享？有什么感受？ / What happened during the meal?
  3. 饭后有什么建议和打算？ / What happened afterwards?
卡住了？查看表达提示 / Need a hint?

请客 → 筷子、示范 → 分享、感受 → 剩菜、建议 → 下次。可以用：先……，吃饭时……，饭后……，因为……所以……。

查看一种参考表达 / One possible answer

上周末，小林请安娜到家里吃中餐。安娜不太会用筷子，所以小林教她怎么用。吃饭时，大家一起分享菜、聊天，安娜觉得很热闹，也尝到了不同的味道。饭后，小林建议把剩菜保存好，避免浪费。安娜同意了，还打算下次请小林吃家乡菜。

表达可以不同；重点是意思清楚、信息准确。Other wording is welcome. Keep the meaning clear and accurate.

自查要点 / Review points

  • 交代人物、时间和邀请 / People, time and invitation
  • 说清筷子的困难和帮助 / Difficulty and help
  • 说清分享食物和安娜的感受 / Sharing and feelings
  • 说清饭后的建议、原因和下次打算 / Advice, reason and next plan

Scenario Dialogue / 情景对话

Explaining dumplings and food customs to a foreign friend. Read it aloud twice: once for meaning, once for fluency — the syllabus tests this task across listening and speaking.

A
你们家为什么喜欢在过年时吃饺子呢？
Nǐmen jiā wèishénme xǐhuan zài guònián shí chī jiǎozi ne?
Why does your family like eating dumplings at Chinese New Year?
B
饺子的样子像古代的钱，代表新的一年发财，全家一起包更热闹。
Jiǎozi de yàngzi xiàng gǔdài de qián, dàibiǎo xīn de yì nián fācái, quánjiā yìqǐ bāo gèng rènao.
Dumplings look like ancient money, symbolizing wealth for the new year — and making them together is part of the fun.
A
我听说不同地方的口味差别很大？
Wǒ tīngshuō bùtóng dìfang de kǒuwèi chābié hěn dà?
I heard flavors differ a lot from region to region?
B
对，各地有不同的口味。不过，同一个地方的人，喜欢吃的东西也不一定一样。
Duì, gèdì yǒu bùtóng de kǒuwèi. Búguò, tóng yí ge dìfang de rén, xǐhuan chī de dōngxi yě bù yídìng yíyàng.
Yes, regional flavors differ. But people from the same place do not necessarily like the same foods.
A
"民以食为天"，中国的饮食文化真是博大精深。
"Mín yǐ shí wéi tiān", Zhōngguó de yǐnshí wénhuà zhēnshi bódà-jīngshēn.
"Food is heaven to the people" — Chinese food culture is truly broad and profound.

Traditional Food Culture FAQ / 常见问题

How many HSK 4 words cover traditional food culture?

This page groups 26 words using this site’s topic tags for traditional food culture. It is a study selection, not an official separate topic word list or a guarantee of comprehension.

Which HSK 4 grammar points are most relevant to traditional food culture?

comparison and measure-words are suggested here for practising traditional food culture contexts. The HSK 4 syllabus expects you to not only understand but also produce these patterns when they involve traditional food culture vocabulary.

What's the difference between HSK 3 and HSK 4 expectations on traditional food culture?

HSK 3 expects simple statements about traditional food culture (e.g., basic facts and short descriptions). HSK 4 raises the bar to "有一定复杂度" — handling extended contexts with opinions, comparisons, and reasoning. You'll need to express feelings (感受) and views (看法) about traditional food culture, not just describe them.
