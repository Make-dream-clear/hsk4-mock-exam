Scenario Dialogue / 情景对话

A couple divides up the housework. Read it aloud twice: once for meaning, once for fluency — the syllabus tests this task across listening and speaking.

A
今天家里要大扫除，咱们分一下工吧。
Jīntiān jiāli yào dàsǎochú, zánmen fēn yíxià gōng ba.
We are doing a big clean-up today — let's divide the chores.
B
行，我负责擦窗户和拖地，你来收拾厨房怎么样？
Xíng, wǒ fùzé cā chuānghu hé tuō dì, nǐ lái shōushi chúfáng zěnmeyàng?
OK, I will do the windows and mop the floor; how about you tidy the kitchen?
A
没问题。对了，妈妈周末要来，得把客房的床单换了。
Méi wèntí. Duì le, māma zhōumò yào lái, děi bǎ kèfáng de chuángdān huàn le.
No problem. Oh, Mom is coming this weekend — we need to change the sheets in the guest room.
B
我来换。晚上想吃什么？我去买菜。
Wǒ lái huàn. Wǎnshang xiǎng chī shénme? Wǒ qù mǎi cài.
I will change them. What do you want for dinner? I will go grocery shopping.
A
做个鱼吧，妈妈爱吃。家务做完我们早点儿休息。
Zuò ge yú ba, māma ài chī. Jiāwù zuò wán wǒmen zǎodiǎnr xiūxi.
Let's make fish — Mom loves it. Once the chores are done we can rest early.

Why HSK 4 Candidates Struggle with Family Life

Discuss home life, family relationships, growing up, habits, and household affairs. Includes topics like parent-child relationships and hometown memories.

After studying the 22 selected words, practise combining them in extended contexts about family life. Use the dialogue to identify a sequence of events, opinions, or reasons, then describe them in your own words.

Key vocabulary to anchor your understanding: 儿童 (értóng, child), 父母 (fùmǔ, parents), 父女 (fùnǚ, father and daughter), 父亲 (fùqīn, father). Practise these words in listening dialogues (听力) and reading passages (阅读), using grammar patterns like complex-sentences and pivotal-sentences.

Family Life FAQ / 常见问题

How many HSK 4 words cover family life?

This page groups 22 words using this site’s topic tags for family life. It is a study selection, not an official separate topic word list or a guarantee of comprehension.

Which HSK 4 grammar points are most relevant to family life?

complex-sentences and pivotal-sentences are suggested here for practising family life contexts. The HSK 4 syllabus expects you to not only understand but also produce these patterns when they involve family life vocabulary.

What's the difference between HSK 3 and HSK 4 expectations on family life?

HSK 3 expects simple statements about family life (e.g., basic facts and short descriptions). HSK 4 raises the bar to "有一定复杂度" — handling extended contexts with opinions, comparisons, and reasoning. You'll need to express feelings (感受) and views (看法) about family life, not just describe them.
