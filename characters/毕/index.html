毕 (bì) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

毕
bì to finish, to conclude, to end; completed 6 strokes · Radical 比 (to compare) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 毕 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

毕 (bì) means to finish, to conclude, to end; completed. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 比 (to compare), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bì.

  • to finish
  • to conclude
  • to end
  • completed

Decomposition

毕 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

毕 =
比 top to compare
十 bottom ten

Radical

The radical of 毕 is 比 — meaning to compare. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

毕 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 十 (complete) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 比 originally indicated the sound.
