既 (jì) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

既
jì already, since, then; both; de facto 9 strokes · Radical 旡 (to choke on something) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 既 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

既 (jì) means already, since, then; both; de facto. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 旡 (to choke on something), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jì.

  • already
  • since
  • then
  • both
  • de facto

Decomposition

既 breaks down into 1 component arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

既 =
旡 left to choke on something

Radical

The radical of 既 is 旡 — meaning to choke on something. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

既 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A person 旡 turning away from food 皀; compare 即.

HSK 4 Words Containing 既

既 jì both... (and...); since; already
这家餐厅的菜既好吃又便宜。
Zhè jiā cāntīng de cài jì hǎochī yòu piányi.
The food at this restaurant is both delicious and cheap.
既然 jìrán since; now that; given that
既然你来了，就多待一会儿。
Jìrán nǐ lái le, jiù duō dāi yíhuìr.
Since you're here, stay a while longer.
