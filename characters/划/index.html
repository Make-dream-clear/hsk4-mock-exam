How to write 划 (huà) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

划
huà to row or paddle a boat; to scratch; to plan; profitable 6 strokes · Radical 刂 (knife) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

划 (huà) means to row or paddle a boat; to scratch; to plan; profitable. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 刂 (knife), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #102 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced huà.

  • to row or paddle a boat
  • to scratch
  • to plan
  • profitable

Decomposition

划 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

Radical

The radical of 划 is 刂 — meaning knife. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 利剧列判剩则.
