彩 (cǎi) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

彩
cǎi color, hue; prize; brilliant; variegated 11 strokes · Radical 彡 (hair) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 彩 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

彩 (cǎi) means color, hue; prize; brilliant; variegated. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 彡 (hair), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced cǎi.

  • color
  • hue
  • prize
  • brilliant
  • variegated

Decomposition

彩 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

彩 =
采 left to collect
彡 right hair

Radical

The radical of 彩 is 彡 — meaning hair. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

彩 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 彡 (hair) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 采 originally indicated the sound.
