肤 (fū) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

肤
fū skin; shallow, superficial 8 strokes · Radical ⺼ (meat) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 肤 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

肤 (fū) means skin; shallow, superficial. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical ⺼ (meat), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced fū.

  • skin
  • shallow
  • superficial

Decomposition

肤 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

肤 =
⺼ left meat
夫 right man

Radical

The radical of 肤 is ⺼ — meaning meat. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 背膊肚膏胳肯脾脱.

How the character is built

肤 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component ⺼ (flesh) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 夫 originally indicated the sound.

Her skin is very fair.
