塑 (sù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

塑
sù to sculpt, to model in clay; plastic 13 strokes · Radical 土 (soil) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 塑 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

塑 (sù) means to sculpt, to model in clay; plastic. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical 土 (soil), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced sù.

  • to sculpt
  • to model in clay
  • plastic

Decomposition

塑 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

塑 =
朔 top beginning
土 bottom soil

Radical

The radical of 塑 is 土 — meaning soil. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 基增堂土堵圾垃填址.

How the character is built

塑 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 土 (earth) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 朔 originally indicated the sound.
