则 (zé) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

则
zé rule, law, regulation; grades 6 strokes · Radical 刂 (knife) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 则 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

则 (zé) means rule, law, regulation; grades. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 刂 (knife), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zé.

  • rule
  • law
  • regulation
  • grades

Decomposition

则 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

则 =
贝 left sea shell
刂 right knife

Radical

The radical of 则 is 刂 — meaning knife. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 划利剧列判剩.

How the character is built

则 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Laws inscribed 刂 on a slate 贝.
