距 (jù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

距
jù distance, gap 11 strokes · Radical 足 (foot) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 距 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

距 (jù) means distance, gap. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 足 (foot), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jù.

  • distance
  • gap

Decomposition

距 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

距 =
足 left foot
巨 right large

Radical

The radical of 距 is 足 — meaning foot. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

距 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 足 (foot) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 巨 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 距
