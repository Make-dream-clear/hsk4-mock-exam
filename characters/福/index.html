福 (fú) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

福
fú happiness, good fortune, blessings 13 strokes · Radical 礻 (spirit) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 福 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

福 (fú) means happiness, good fortune, blessings. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical 礻 (spirit), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced fú.

  • happiness
  • good fortune
  • blessings

Decomposition

福 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

福 =
礻 left spirit
畐 right to fill

Radical

The radical of 福 is 礻 — meaning spirit. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 社祝.

How the character is built

福 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 礻 (spirit) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 畐 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 福
