How to write 验 (yàn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

验
yàn to examine, to inspect, to test, to verify 10 strokes · Radical 马 (horse) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

验 (yàn) means to examine, to inspect, to test, to verify. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 马 (horse), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #116 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yàn.

  • to examine
  • to inspect
  • to test
  • to verify

Decomposition

验 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

验 =
马 left horse
佥 right all

Radical

The radical of 验 is 马 — meaning horse. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 骄.

How the character is built

验 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 马 (horse) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 佥 originally indicated the sound.
