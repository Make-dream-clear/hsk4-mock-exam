幸 (xìng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

幸
xìng favor, fortune, luck; surname 8 strokes · Radical 干 (arid) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 幸 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

幸 (xìng) means favor, fortune, luck; surname. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 干 (arid), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xìng.

  • favor
  • fortune
  • luck
  • surname

Decomposition

幸 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

幸 = 土 top soil
丷 bottom kwukyel
干 part arid

Radical

The radical of 幸 is 干 — meaning arid. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 并.

How the character is built

幸 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Two men, one alive 土 and one dead 干; the living one is lucky.
