貌 (mào) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

貌
mào countenance, appearance 14 strokes · Radical 豸 (badger) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 貌 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

貌 (mào) means countenance, appearance. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 豸 (badger), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced mào.

  • countenance
  • appearance

Decomposition

貌 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

貌 =
豸 left badger
皃 right countenance

Radical

The radical of 貌 is 豸 — meaning badger. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

貌 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A beast's 豸 appearance 皃; 皃 also provides the pronunciation.
