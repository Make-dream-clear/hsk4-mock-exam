剧 (jù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

剧
jù theatrical plays, opera, drama; severe, acute 10 strokes · Radical 刂 (knife) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 剧 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

剧 (jù) means theatrical plays, opera, drama; severe, acute. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 刂 (knife), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jù.

  • theatrical plays
  • opera
  • drama
  • severe
  • acute

Decomposition

剧 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

剧 =
居 left to live
刂 right knife

Radical

The radical of 剧 is 刂 — meaning knife. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 划利列判剩则.

How the character is built

剧 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 刂 (knife) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 居 originally indicated the sound.
