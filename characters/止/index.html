How to write 止 (zhǐ) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

止
zhǐ to stop, to halt; to detain; to desist 4 strokes · Radical 止 (to stop) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

止 (zhǐ) means to stop, to halt; to detain; to desist. It is written in 4 strokes, with the radical 止 (to stop), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #81 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhǐ.

  • to stop
  • to halt
  • to detain
  • to desist

Radical

The radical of 止 is 止 — meaning to stop. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 此.

How the character is built

止 is a pictograph — a stylized image of the thing it names. It originally depicted A foot.

HSK 4 Words Containing 止

禁止 jìnzhǐ to prohibit; to ban
博物馆里禁止拍照。
Bówùguǎn lǐ jìnzhǐ pāizhào.
Photography is prohibited in the museum.
停止 tíngzhǐ to stop; to cease
雨已经停止了。
Yǔ yǐjīng tíngzhǐ le.
The rain has already stopped.
