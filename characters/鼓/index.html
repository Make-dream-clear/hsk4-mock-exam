鼓 (gǔ) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

鼓
gǔ drum; to beat, to strike; to rouse 13 strokes · Radical 鼓 (drum) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 鼓 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

鼓 (gǔ) means drum; to beat, to strike; to rouse. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical 鼓 (drum), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced gǔ.

  • drum
  • to beat
  • to strike
  • to rouse

Decomposition

鼓 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

鼓 =
壴 left drum
支 right to support

Radical

The radical of 鼓 is 鼓 — meaning drum. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

鼓 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 壴 (drum) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 支 originally indicated the sound.
