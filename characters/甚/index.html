甚 (shén) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

甚
shén considerably; very, extremely; a great extent 9 strokes · Radical 甘 (sweet) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 甚 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

甚 (shén) means considerably; very, extremely; a great extent. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 甘 (sweet), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shén.

  • considerably
  • very
  • extremely
  • a great extent

甚 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

甚 =

Radical

The radical of 甚 is 甘 — meaning sweet. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.
