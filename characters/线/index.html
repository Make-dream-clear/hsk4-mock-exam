How to write 线 (xiàn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

线
xiàn line, thread, wire; clue, trail 8 strokes · Radical 纟 (silk) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

线 (xiàn) means line, thread, wire; clue, trail. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 纟 (silk), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #57 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xiàn.

  • line
  • thread
  • wire
  • clue
  • trail

Decomposition

线 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

线 =
纟 left silk
戋 right small

Radical

The radical of 线 is 纟 — meaning silk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 约组纪继绝细续.

How the character is built

线 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 纟 (thread) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 戋 originally indicated the sound.
