How to write 专 (zhuān) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

专
zhuān concentrated, specialized; to monopolize 4 strokes · Radical 一 (one) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

专 (zhuān) means concentrated, specialized; to monopolize. It is written in 4 strokes, with the radical 一 (one), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #91 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhuān.

  • concentrated
  • specialized
  • to monopolize

Radical

The radical of 专 is 一 — meaning one. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 亚与严.

HSK 4 Words Containing 专

专门 zhuānmén specially; specifically; specialized
我专门来找你的。
Wǒ zhuānmén lái zhǎo nǐ de.
I came specifically to find you.
专业 zhuānyè major; specialty; specialized
她的专业是计算机科学。
Tā de zhuānyè shì jìsuànjī kēxué.
Her major is computer science.
