待 (dài) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

待
dài to entertain, to receive, to treat; to delay, to wait 9 strokes · Radical 彳 (to step with the left foot) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 待 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

待 (dài) means to entertain, to receive, to treat; to delay, to wait. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 彳 (to step with the left foot), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced dài.

  • to entertain
  • to receive
  • to treat
  • to delay
  • to wait

Decomposition

待 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

待 =
彳 left to step with the left foot
寺 right court

Radical

The radical of 待 is 彳 — meaning to step with the left foot. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 律微.

How the character is built

待 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. To welcome someone arriving 彳 at the royal court 寺.

HSK 4 Words Containing 待

待 dāi to stay; to remain
他在家里待了一整天。
Tā zài jiā lǐ dāi le yì zhěng tiān.
He stayed at home for a whole day.
