列 (liè) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

列
liè a line; to arrange, to classify 6 strokes · Radical 刂 (knife) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 列 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

列 (liè) means a line; to arrange, to classify. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 刂 (knife), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced liè.

  • a line
  • to arrange
  • to classify

Decomposition

列 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

列 =
歹 left corpse
刂 right knife

Radical

The radical of 列 is 刂 — meaning knife. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 划利剧判剩则.
