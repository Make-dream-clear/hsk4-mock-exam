谊 (yì) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

谊
yì friendship; appropriate, suitable 10 strokes · Radical 讠 (words) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 谊 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

谊 (yì) means friendship; appropriate, suitable. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 讠 (words), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yì.

  • friendship
  • appropriate
  • suitable

Decomposition

谊 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

谊 =
讠 left words
宜 right fitting

Radical

The radical of 谊 is 讠 — meaning words. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 误计许谈讨论诚订谅评详译.

How the character is built

谊 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Fitting 宜 speech 讠; 宜 also provides the pronunciation.
