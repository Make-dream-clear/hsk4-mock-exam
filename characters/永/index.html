How to write 永 (yǒng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

永
yǒng long; perpetual, eternal; forever 5 strokes · Radical 水 (water) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

永 (yǒng) means long; perpetual, eternal; forever. It is written in 5 strokes, with the radical 水 (water), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #121 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yǒng.

  • long
  • perpetual
  • eternal
  • forever
