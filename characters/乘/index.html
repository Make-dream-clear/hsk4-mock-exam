乘 (chéng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

乘
chéng ride, mount; to make use of; to ascend; to multiply 10 strokes · Radical 丿 (slash) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 乘 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

乘 (chéng) means ride, mount; to make use of; to ascend; to multiply. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 丿 (slash), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced chéng.

  • ride
  • mount
  • to make use of
  • to ascend
  • to multiply

Decomposition

乘 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Overlap. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

乘 =
禾 back cereal
北 front north

Radical

The radical of 乘 is 丿 — meaning slash. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 之乓乒.

How the character is built

乘 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Two feet 北 climbing a beanstalk 禾.

HSK 4 Words Containing 乘

乘1 chéng to ride (a vehicle)
他每天乘地铁上班。
Tā měitiān chéng dìtiě shàngbān.
He takes the subway to work every day.
乘客 chéngkè passenger
乘客们正在排队上车。
Chéngkèmen zhèngzài páiduì shàng chē.
Passengers are lining up to board.
乘坐 chéngzuò to travel by; to ride
请乘坐电梯到三楼。
Qǐng chéngzuò diàntī dào sān lóu.
Please take the elevator to the third floor.
换乘 huànchéng to transfer (transit)
在这一站换乘二号线。
Zài zhè yí zhàn huànchéng èr hào xiàn.
Transfer to Line 2 at this station.
