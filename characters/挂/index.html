How to write 挂 (guà) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

挂
guà to suspend, to put up, to hang; suspense 9 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

挂 (guà) means to suspend, to put up, to hang; suspense. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #145 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced guà.

  • to suspend
  • to put up
  • to hang
  • suspense

Decomposition

挂 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

挂 =
扌 left hand
圭 right jade tablet

Radical

The radical of 挂 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔掉拉指推排按播擦操.

How the character is built

挂 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 圭 originally indicated the sound.
