馒 (mán) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

馒
mán steamed buns; steamed dumplings 14 strokes · Radical 饣 (food) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 馒 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

馒 (mán) means steamed buns; steamed dumplings. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 饣 (food), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced mán.

  • steamed buns
  • steamed dumplings

Decomposition

馒 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

馒 =
饣 left food
曼 right long

Radical

The radical of 馒 is 饣 — meaning food. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 饼.

How the character is built

馒 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 饣 (food) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 曼 originally indicated the sound.
