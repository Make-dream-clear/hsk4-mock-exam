How to write 村 (cūn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

村
cūn village, hamlet; vulgar, uncouth 7 strokes · Radical 木 (tree) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

村 (cūn) means village, hamlet; vulgar, uncouth. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 木 (tree), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #69 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced cūn.

  • village
  • hamlet
  • vulgar
  • uncouth

Decomposition

村 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

村 =
木 left tree
寸 right inch

Radical

The radical of 村 is 木 — meaning tree. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 标术案棒材格棵林梦桥染森.

How the character is built

村 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 木 (tree) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 寸 originally indicated the sound.
