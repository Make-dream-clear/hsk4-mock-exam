棒 (bàng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

棒
bàng stick, club; strong, smart; to hit 12 strokes · Radical 木 (tree) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 棒 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

棒 (bàng) means stick, club; strong, smart; to hit. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 木 (tree), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bàng.

  • stick
  • club
  • strong
  • smart
  • to hit

Decomposition

棒 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

棒 =
木 left tree
奉 right to offer

Radical

The radical of 棒 is 木 — meaning tree. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 标村术案材格棵林梦桥染森.

How the character is built

棒 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 木 (wood) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 奉 originally indicated the sound.
