翻 (fān) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

翻
fān to upset, to capsize, to flip over 18 strokes · Radical 羽 (feather) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 翻 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

翻 (fān) means to upset, to capsize, to flip over. It is written in 18 strokes, with the radical 羽 (feather), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced fān.

  • to upset
  • to capsize
  • to flip over

Decomposition

翻 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

翻 =
番 left to take turns
羽 right feather

Radical

The radical of 翻 is 羽 — meaning feather. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

翻 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 羽 (wings) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 番 originally indicated the sound.
