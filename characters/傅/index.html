傅 (fù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

傅
fù tutor, teacher; to assist; surname 12 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 傅 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

傅 (fù) means tutor, teacher; to assist; surname. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced fù.

  • tutor
  • teacher
  • to assist
  • surname

Decomposition

傅 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

傅 =
亻 left man
尃 right to announce

Radical

The radical of 傅 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 保任使倒低例俩份停傲倍付.

How the character is built

傅 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Someone 亻 who lectures 尃; 尃 also provides the pronunciation.
