How to write 吹 (chuī) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

吹
chuī to blow, to puff; to brag, to boast 7 strokes · Radical 口 (mouth) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

吹 (chuī) means to blow, to puff; to brag, to boast. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 口 (mouth), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #117 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced chuī.

  • to blow
  • to puff
  • to brag
  • to boast

Decomposition

吹 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

吹 =
口 left mouth
欠 right to lack

Radical

The radical of 吹 is 口 — meaning mouth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 品台否另各味喊咳呼命嗯售.

How the character is built

吹 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 口 (mouth) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 欠 originally indicated the sound.
