脾 (pí) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

脾
pí spleen, pancreas; temper, disposition 12 strokes · Radical ⺼ (meat) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 脾 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

脾 (pí) means spleen, pancreas; temper, disposition. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical ⺼ (meat), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced pí.

  • spleen
  • pancreas
  • temper
  • disposition

Decomposition

脾 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

脾 =
⺼ left meat
卑 right humble

Radical

The radical of 脾 is ⺼ — meaning meat. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 背膊肚肤膏胳肯脱.

How the character is built

脾 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component ⺼ (flesh) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 卑 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 脾

脾气 píqi temperament; temper
他的脾气有时候不太好。
Tā de píqi yǒu shíhou bú tài hǎo.
His temper is sometimes not very good.
