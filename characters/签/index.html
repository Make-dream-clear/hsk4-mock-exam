签 (qiān) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

签
qiān to sign, to endorse; a note, a slip of paper 13 strokes · Radical ⺮ (bamboo) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 签 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

签 (qiān) means to sign, to endorse; a note, a slip of paper. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical ⺮ (bamboo), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced qiān.

  • to sign
  • to endorse
  • a note
  • a slip of paper

Decomposition

签 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

签 =
⺮ top bamboo
佥 bottom all

Radical

The radical of 签 is ⺮ — meaning bamboo. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 篇管笨符籍.

How the character is built

签 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component ⺮ (bamboo) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 佥 originally indicated the sound.
