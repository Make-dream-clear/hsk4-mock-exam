How to write 技 (jì) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

技
jì ability, talent; skill, technique 7 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

技 (jì) means ability, talent; skill, technique. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #63 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jì.

  • ability
  • talent
  • skill
  • technique

Decomposition

技 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

技 =
扌 left hand
支 right to support

Radical

The radical of 技 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱扔掉拉指挂推排按播擦操.

How the character is built

技 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 支 originally indicated the sound.

科技的发展改变了生活。
Kējì de fāzhǎn gǎibiàn le shēnghuó.
