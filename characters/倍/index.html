倍 (bèi) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

倍
bèi to double; to increase or multiply 10 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 倍 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

倍 (bèi) means to double; to increase or multiply. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bèi.

  • to double
  • to increase or multiply

Decomposition

倍 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

倍 =
亻 left man
咅 right to spit out

Radical

The radical of 倍 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 保任使倒低例俩份停傲付傅.

HSK 4 Words Containing 倍

倍 bèi times; -fold (multiplier)
今年的销售量是去年的两倍。
Jīnnián de xiāoshòuliàng shì qùnián de liǎng bèi.
This year's sales are double last year's.
