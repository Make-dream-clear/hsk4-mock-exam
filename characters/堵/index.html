堵 (dǔ) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

堵
dǔ wall; to stop, to prevent, to block 11 strokes · Radical 土 (soil) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 堵 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

堵 (dǔ) means wall; to stop, to prevent, to block. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 土 (soil), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced dǔ.

  • wall
  • to stop
  • to prevent
  • to block

Decomposition

堵 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

堵 = 土 left soil
者 right that which

Radical

The radical of 堵 is 土 — meaning soil. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 基增堂土圾垃塑填址.

How the character is built

堵 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 土 (earth) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 者 originally indicated the sound.
