懒 (lǎn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

懒
lǎn lazy, languid, listless 16 strokes · Radical 忄 (heart) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 懒 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

懒 (lǎn) means lazy, languid, listless. It is written in 16 strokes, with the radical 忄 (heart), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced lǎn.

  • lazy
  • languid
  • listless

Decomposition

懒 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

懒 =
忄 left heart
赖 right to depend on

Radical

The radical of 懒 is 忄 — meaning heart. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 性怀悔惊恼惜忆愉.

How the character is built

懒 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 忄 (heart) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 赖 originally indicated the sound.
