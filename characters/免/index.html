免 (miǎn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

免
miǎn to spare, to excuse from; to evade 7 strokes · Radical 儿 (son) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 免 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

免 (miǎn) means to spare, to excuse from; to evade. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 儿 (son), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced miǎn.

  • to spare
  • to excuse from
  • to evade

Radical

The radical of 免 is 儿 — meaning son. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 兄克光允.
