垃 (lā) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

垃
lā garbage, refuse, trash, waste 8 strokes · Radical 土 (soil) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 垃 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

垃 (lā) means garbage, refuse, trash, waste. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 土 (soil), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced lā.

  • garbage
  • refuse
  • trash
  • waste

Decomposition

垃 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

垃 = 土 left soil
立 right to stand

Radical

The radical of 垃 is 土 — meaning soil. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 基增堂土堵圾塑填址.

How the character is built

垃 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 土 (earth) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 立 originally indicated the sound.
