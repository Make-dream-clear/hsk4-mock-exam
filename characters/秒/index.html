秒 (miǎo) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

秒
miǎo a second; a corn kernel; measure word for time 9 strokes · Radical 禾 (cereal) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 秒 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

秒 (miǎo) means a second; a corn kernel; measure word for time. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 禾 (cereal), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced miǎo.

  • a second
  • a corn kernel
  • measure word for time

Decomposition

秒 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

秒 =
禾 left cereal
少 right few

Radical

The radical of 秒 is 禾 — meaning cereal. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 程科积稍秀.

How the character is built

秒 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 禾 (grain) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 少 originally indicated the sound.
