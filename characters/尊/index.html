尊 (zūn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

尊
zūn to honor, to respect, to venerate 12 strokes · Radical 寸 (inch) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 尊 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

尊 (zūn) means to honor, to respect, to venerate. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 寸 (inch), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zūn.

  • to honor
  • to respect
  • to venerate

Decomposition

尊 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

尊 =
酋 top chief of tribe
寸 bottom inch

Radical

The radical of 尊 is 寸 — meaning inch. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 导.

How the character is built

尊 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A hand 寸 making an offering of wine 酋.

HSK 4 Words Containing 尊

Wǒmen yào zūnzhòng měi gè rén de xiǎngfǎ.
We should respect everyone's ideas.
