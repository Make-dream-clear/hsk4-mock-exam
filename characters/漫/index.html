漫 (màn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

漫
màn inundating; exaggerated 14 strokes · Radical 氵 (water) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 漫 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

漫 (màn) means inundating; exaggerated. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 氵 (water), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced màn.

  • inundating
  • exaggerated

Decomposition

漫 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

漫 =
氵 left water
曼 right long

Radical

The radical of 漫 is 氵 — meaning water. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 济江深油汤流温汗浪泼污消.

How the character is built

漫 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 氵 (water) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 曼 originally indicated the sound.
