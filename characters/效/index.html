效 (xiào) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

效
xiào result, effect; effective 10 strokes · Radical 攵 (to rap) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 效 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

效 (xiào) means result, effect; effective. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 攵 (to rap), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xiào.

  • result
  • effect
  • effective

Decomposition

效 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

效 =
交 left to connect
攵 right to rap

Radical

The radical of 效 is 攵 — meaning to rap. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 改敢散整.

How the character is built

效 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 攵 (strike) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 交 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 效

效果 xiàoguǒ effect; result; outcome
这种药的效果很好。
Zhè zhǒng yào de xiàoguǒ hěn hǎo.
This medicine works very well.
有效 yǒuxiào valid; effective
这个办法非常有效。
Zhège bànfǎ fēicháng yǒuxiào.
This method is very effective.
