脱 (tuō) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

脱
tuō to take off, to shed; to escape from 11 strokes · Radical ⺼ (meat) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 脱 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

脱 (tuō) means to take off, to shed; to escape from. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical ⺼ (meat), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tuō.

  • to take off
  • to shed
  • to escape from

Decomposition

脱 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

脱 =
⺼ left meat
兑 right cash

Radical

The radical of 脱 is ⺼ — meaning meat. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 背膊肚肤膏胳肯脾.

How the character is built

脱 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component ⺼ (flesh) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 兑 originally indicated the sound.
