How to write 克 (kè) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

克
kè to subdue, to restrain, to overcome; used in transliterations 7 strokes · Radical 儿 (son) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

克 (kè) means to subdue, to restrain, to overcome; used in transliterations. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 儿 (son), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #42 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced kè.

  • to subdue
  • to restrain
  • to overcome
  • used in transliterations

Decomposition

克 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

克 =
十 top ten
兄 bottom elder brother

Radical

The radical of 克 is 儿 — meaning son. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 兄光免允.

How the character is built

克 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A weapon 十 used to subdue a beast 兄.
