闹 (nào) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

闹
nào busy, lively; to dispute, to quarrel 8 strokes · Radical 门 (gate) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 闹 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

闹 (nào) means busy, lively; to dispute, to quarrel. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 门 (gate), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced nào.

  • busy
  • lively
  • to dispute
  • to quarrel

Decomposition

闹 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Outer (open below) + Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

闹 =
门 outer gate
市 inner city

Radical

The radical of 闹 is 门 — meaning gate. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 阅.

How the character is built

闹 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. As busy as the market 市 gate 门.
