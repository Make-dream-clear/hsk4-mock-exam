肚 (dù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

肚
dù belly; abdomen, bowels 7 strokes · Radical ⺼ (meat) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 肚 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

肚 (dù) means belly; abdomen, bowels. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical ⺼ (meat), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced dù.

  • belly
  • abdomen
  • bowels

Decomposition

肚 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

肚 =
⺼ left meat
土 right soil

Radical

The radical of 肚 is ⺼ — meaning meat. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 背膊肤膏胳肯脾脱.

How the character is built

肚 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component ⺼ (flesh) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 土 originally indicated the sound.
