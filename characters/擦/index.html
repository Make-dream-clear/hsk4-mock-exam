擦 (cā) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

擦
cā to clean, to erase, to polish; a brush 17 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 擦 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

擦 (cā) means to clean, to erase, to polish; a brush. It is written in 17 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced cā.

  • to clean
  • to erase
  • to polish
  • a brush

Decomposition

擦 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

擦 =
扌 left hand
察 right to examine

Radical

The radical of 擦 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔掉拉指挂推排按播操.

How the character is built

擦 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 察 originally indicated the sound.
