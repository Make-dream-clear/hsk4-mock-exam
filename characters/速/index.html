速 (sù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

速
sù prompt, quick, speedy 10 strokes · Radical 辶 (to walk) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 速 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

速 (sù) means prompt, quick, speedy. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 辶 (to walk), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced sù.

  • prompt
  • quick
  • speedy

Decomposition

速 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Lower-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

速 =
辶 outer to walk
束 inner to bind

Radical

The radical of 速 is 辶 — meaning to walk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 达连通递逛迷邀.

How the character is built

速 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 辶 (walk) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 束 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 速

车速 chēsù vehicle speed
在学校附近要降低车速。
Zài xuéxiào fùjìn yào jiàngdī chēsù.
You should reduce speed near schools.
高速 gāosù high-speed
高速公路上车辆很多。
Gāosù gōnglù shàng chēliàng hěn duō.
There are many vehicles on the highway.
快速 kuàisù fast; quick; rapid
高铁是一种快速的交通方式。
Gāotiě shì yì zhǒng kuàisù de jiāotōng fāngshì.
High-speed rail is a fast mode of transportation.
速度 sùdù speed
请降低行驶速度。
Qǐng jiàngdī xíngshǐ sùdù.
Please reduce your driving speed.
