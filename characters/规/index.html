规 (guī) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

规
guī rules, regulations, customs, law 8 strokes · Radical 见 (to see) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 规 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

规 (guī) means rules, regulations, customs, law. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 见 (to see), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced guī.

  • rules
  • regulations
  • customs
  • law

Decomposition

规 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

规 =
夫 left man
见 right to see

Radical

The radical of 规 is 见 — meaning to see. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 观.

How the character is built

规 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 见 (observe) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 夫 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 规

规定 guīdìng to regulate; regulation; rule
公司规定上班不能迟到。
Gōngsī guīdìng shàngbān bù néng chídào.
The company rule says you can't be late for work.
