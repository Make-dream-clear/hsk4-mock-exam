鼻 (bí) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

鼻
bí nose; first 14 strokes · Radical 鼻 (nose) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 鼻 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

鼻 (bí) means nose; first. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 鼻 (nose), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bí.

  • nose
  • first

Decomposition

鼻 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

鼻 =
自 top self
畀 bottom to give

Radical

The radical of 鼻 is 鼻 — meaning nose. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

鼻 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 自 (self) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 畀 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 鼻

鼻子 bízi nose
他的鼻子很高。
Tā de bízi hěn gāo.
He has a high nose bridge.
