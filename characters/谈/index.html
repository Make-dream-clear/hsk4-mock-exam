How to write 谈 (tán) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

谈
tán to talk, to chat; conversation; surname 10 strokes · Radical 讠 (words) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

谈 (tán) means to talk, to chat; conversation; surname. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 讠 (words), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #138 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tán.

  • to talk
  • to chat
  • conversation
  • surname

Decomposition

谈 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

谈 =
讠 left words
炎 right flame

Radical

The radical of 谈 is 讠 — meaning words. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 误计许讨论诚订谅评详译谊.

How the character is built

谈 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 讠 (speech) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 炎 originally indicated the sound.
