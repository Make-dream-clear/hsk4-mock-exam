申 (shēn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

申
shēn to report; to extend; to explain; to declare 5 strokes · Radical 田 (field) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 申 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

申 (shēn) means to report; to extend; to explain; to declare. It is written in 5 strokes, with the radical 田 (field), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shēn.

  • to report
  • to extend
  • to explain
  • to declare

Decomposition

申 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Overlap. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

申 =
日 back sun
丨 front number one

Radical

The radical of 申 is 田 — meaning field. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 由.

How the character is built

申 is a pictograph — a stylized image of the thing it names. It originally depicted A bolt of lightning.
