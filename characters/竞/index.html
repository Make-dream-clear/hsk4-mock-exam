竞 (jìng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

竞
jìng to compete, to contend, to vie 10 strokes · Radical 立 (to stand) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 竞 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

竞 (jìng) means to compete, to contend, to vie. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 立 (to stand), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jìng.

  • to compete
  • to contend
  • to vie

Decomposition

竞 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

竞 =
立 top to stand
兄 bottom elder brother

Radical

The radical of 竞 is 立 — meaning to stand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 章竟亲童.

How the character is built

竞 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. To stand up 立 to a foe 兄.

HSK 4 Words Containing 竞

Zhège hángyè de jìngzhēng fēicháng jīliè.
Competition in this industry is very fierce.
