How to write 够 (gòu) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

够
gòu enough, adequate; to reach, to attain; to pass muster 11 strokes · Radical 夕 (evening) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

够 (gòu) means enough, adequate; to reach, to attain; to pass muster. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 夕 (evening), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #51 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced gòu.

  • enough
  • adequate
  • to reach
  • to attain
  • to pass muster

Decomposition

够 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

够 =
句 left sentence
多 right much

Radical

The radical of 够 is 夕 — meaning evening. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 夜.

How the character is built

够 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 多 (more) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 句 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 够

不够 búgòu not enough; insufficient
时间不够用了。
Shíjiān búgòu yòng le.
There isn't enough time.
够 gòu enough; sufficient
这些钱够买一本书。
Zhèxiē qián gòu mǎi yì běn shū.
This money is enough to buy a book.
能够 nénggòu to be able to; can
我相信你能够做到。
Wǒ xiāngxìn nǐ nénggòu zuòdào.
I believe you can do it.
