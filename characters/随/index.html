How to write 随 (suí) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

随
suí to follow, to listen to, to submit to 11 strokes · Radical 阝 (place) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

随 (suí) means to follow, to listen to, to submit to. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 阝 (place), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #78 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced suí.

  • to follow
  • to listen to
  • to submit to

Decomposition

随 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

随 =
阝 left place
迶 right to walk

Radical

The radical of 随 is 阝 — meaning place. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 部际队降郊陪险.

How the character is built

随 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. To walk 迶 to the city 阝; to follow a path.

HSK 4 Words Containing 随

随便坐吧。

Sit anywhere you like.

As time goes by, his Chinese gets better and better.
