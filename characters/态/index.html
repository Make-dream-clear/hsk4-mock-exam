How to write 态 (tài) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

态
tài manner, bearing, attitude 8 strokes · Radical 心 (heart) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

态 (tài) means manner, bearing, attitude. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 心 (heart), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #136 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tài.

  • manner
  • bearing
  • attitude

Decomposition

态 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

态 =
太 top very
心 bottom heart

Radical

The radical of 态 is 心 — meaning heart. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 忽志恐虑悉.

How the character is built

态 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 心 (heart) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 太 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 态

态度 tàidù attitude; manner
服务员的态度很好。
Fúwùyuán de tàidù hěn hǎo.
The waiter's attitude was very good.
