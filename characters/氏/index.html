氏 (shì) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

氏
shì clan, family; maiden name 4 strokes · Radical 氏 (clan) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 氏 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

氏 (shì) means clan, family; maiden name. It is written in 4 strokes, with the radical 氏 (clan), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shì.

  • clan
  • family
  • maiden name

Radical

The radical of 氏 is 氏 — meaning clan. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

氏 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A person bowing down.

HSK 4 Words Containing 氏

今天气温三十摄氏度。
Jīntiān qìwēn sānshí shèshìdù.
The temperature today is 30 degrees Celsius.
