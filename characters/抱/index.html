How to write 抱 (bào) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

抱
bào to embrace, to hold in one's arms; to enfold 8 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

抱 (bào) means to embrace, to hold in one's arms; to enfold. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #56 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bào.

  • to embrace
  • to hold in one's arms
  • to enfold

Decomposition

抱 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

抱 =
扌 left hand
包 right wrap

Radical

The radical of 抱 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 技扔掉拉指挂推排按播擦操.

How the character is built

抱 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. To wrap 包 in one's arms 扌; 包 also provides the pronunciation.

HSK 4 Words Containing 抱

抱 bào to hug; to hold
妈妈抱着孩子走了。
Māma bào zhe háizi zǒu le.
Mom walked away holding the child.

非常抱歉，让你久等了。
Fēicháng bàoqiàn, ràng nǐ jiǔ děng le.
Very sorry to have kept you waiting.
