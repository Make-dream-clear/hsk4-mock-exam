How to write 汤 (tāng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

汤
tāng soup, gravy, broth; hot water 6 strokes · Radical 氵 (water) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

汤 (tāng) means soup, gravy, broth; hot water. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 氵 (water), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #139 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tāng.

  • soup
  • gravy
  • broth
  • hot water

Decomposition

汤 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

汤 =
氵 left water
昜 right bright

Radical

The radical of 汤 is 氵 — meaning water. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 济江深油流温汗浪漫泼污消.

How the character is built

汤 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 氵 (water) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 昜 originally indicated the sound.
