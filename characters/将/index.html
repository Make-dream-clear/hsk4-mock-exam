How to write 将 (jiāng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

将
jiāng the future, what will be; ready, prepared; a general 9 strokes · Radical 丬 (half of tree trunk) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

将 (jiāng) means the future, what will be; ready, prepared; a general. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 丬 (half of tree trunk), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #37 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jiāng.

  • the future
  • what will be
  • ready
  • prepared
  • a general

Decomposition

将 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

将 =
丬 left half of tree trunk
夕 right evening
寸 part inch

Radical

The radical of 将 is 丬 — meaning half of tree trunk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

将 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Going to bed 丬 at night 夕, a predictable future.

HSK 4 Words Containing 将

将 jiāng will; about to (formal); by (prep)
会议将在下周举行。
Huìyì jiāng zài xià zhōu jǔxíng.
The meeting will be held next week.
将来 jiānglái the future; future
将来你想做什么工作？
Jiānglái nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme gōngzuò?
What kind of job do you want in the future?
将要 jiāngyào will; about to
飞机将要降落。
Fēijī jiāngyào jiàngluò.
The plane is about to land.
