How to write 并 (bìng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

并
bìng to combine, to annex; also, what's more 6 strokes · Radical 干 (arid) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

并 (bìng) means to combine, to annex; also, what's more. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 干 (arid), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #61 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bìng.

  • to combine
  • to annex
  • also
  • what's more

Decomposition

并 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

并 =
丷 top kwukyel
开 bottom to open

Radical

The radical of 并 is 干 — meaning arid. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 幸.

How the character is built

并 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Simplified form of 並; two men standing side-by-side.

HSK 4 Words Containing 并

并 bìng actually; (not) at all; and; moreover
我并不觉得累。
Wǒ bìng bù juéde lèi.
I don't feel tired at all.
并且 bìngqiě and; furthermore; moreover
她聪明并且努力。
Tā cōngming bìngqiě nǔlì.
She is smart and hardworking.
