预 (yù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

预
yù to prepare, to arrange; in advance 10 strokes · Radical 页 (page) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 预 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

预 (yù) means to prepare, to arrange; in advance. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 页 (page), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yù.

  • to prepare
  • to arrange
  • in advance

Decomposition

预 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

预 =
予 left to give
页 right page

Radical

The radical of 预 is 页 — meaning page. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 顺顿频项.

How the character is built

预 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 页 (page) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 予 originally indicated the sound.
