How to write 众 (zhòng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

众
zhòng multitude, crowd; masses, public 6 strokes · Radical 人 (man) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

众 (zhòng) means multitude, crowd; masses, public. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 人 (man), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #89 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhòng.

  • multitude
  • crowd
  • masses
  • public

Decomposition

众 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

众 =
人 top man
人 bottom man
人 part man

Radical

The radical of 众 is 人 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

众 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Three people 人 representing a crowd.
