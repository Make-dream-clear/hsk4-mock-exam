恐 (kǒng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

恐
kǒng fearful, apprehensive; to fear, to dread 10 strokes · Radical 心 (heart) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 恐 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

恐 (kǒng) means fearful, apprehensive; to fear, to dread. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 心 (heart), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced kǒng.

  • fearful
  • apprehensive
  • to fear
  • to dread

Decomposition

恐 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

恐 =
巩 top to bind
心 bottom heart

Radical

The radical of 恐 is 心 — meaning heart. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 忽态志虑悉.

How the character is built

恐 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 心 (heart) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 巩 originally indicated the sound.
