航 (háng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

航
háng vessel, craft; to sail, to navigate 10 strokes · Radical 舟 (boat) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 航 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

航 (háng) means vessel, craft; to sail, to navigate. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 舟 (boat), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced háng.

  • vessel
  • craft
  • to sail
  • to navigate

Decomposition

航 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

航 =
舟 left boat
亢 right high

Radical

The radical of 航 is 舟 — meaning boat. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

航 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 舟 (ship) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 亢 originally indicated the sound.
