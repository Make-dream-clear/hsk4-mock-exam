钢 (gāng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

钢
gāng steel; hard, strong, tough 9 strokes · Radical 钅 (gold) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 钢 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

钢 (gāng) means steel; hard, strong, tough. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 钅 (gold), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced gāng.

  • steel
  • hard
  • strong
  • tough

Decomposition

钢 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

钢 =
钅 left gold
冈 right ridge or crest of hill

Radical

The radical of 钢 is 钅 — meaning gold. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 键镜钥针.

How the character is built

钢 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 钅 (metal) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 冈 originally indicated the sound.
