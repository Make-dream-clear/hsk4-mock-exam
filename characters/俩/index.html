How to write 俩 (liǎ) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

俩
liǎ two, a pair, a couple 9 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

俩 (liǎ) means two, a pair, a couple. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #135 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced liǎ.

  • two
  • a pair
  • a couple

Decomposition

俩 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

俩 =
亻 left man
两 right two

Radical

The radical of 俩 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 保任使倒低例份停傲倍付傅.

How the character is built

俩 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A couple 寿; 寿 also provides the pronunciation.
