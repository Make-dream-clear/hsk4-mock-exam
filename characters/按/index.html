按 (àn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

按
àn to check, to control, to push, to restrain 9 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 按 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

按 (àn) means to check, to control, to push, to restrain. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced àn.

  • to check
  • to control
  • to push
  • to restrain

Decomposition

按 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

按 =
扌 left hand
安 right peaceful

Radical

The radical of 按 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔掉拉指挂推排播擦操.

How the character is built

按 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 安 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 按

按 àn to press; according to
请按这个按钮。
Qǐng àn zhège ànniǔ.
Please press this button.
按时 ànshí on time; punctually
我们要按时完成作业。
Wǒmen yào ànshí wánchéng zuòyè.
We need to finish homework on time.
按照 ànzhào according to; in line with
请按照要求写报告。
Qǐng ànzhào yāoqiú xiě bàogào.
Please write the report according to the requirements.
