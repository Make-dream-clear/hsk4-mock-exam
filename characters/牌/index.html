How to write 牌 (pái) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

牌
pái card, game piece; placard, signboard, tablet 12 strokes · Radical 片 (slice) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

牌 (pái) means card, game piece; placard, signboard, tablet. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 片 (slice), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #54 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced pái.

  • card
  • game piece
  • placard
  • signboard
  • tablet

Decomposition

牌 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

牌 =
片 left slice
卑 right humble

Radical

The radical of 牌 is 片 — meaning slice. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

牌 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 片 (page) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 卑 originally indicated the sound.
