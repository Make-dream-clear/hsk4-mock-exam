奖 (jiǎng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

奖
jiǎng prize, reward; to award 9 strokes · Radical 大 (big) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 奖 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

奖 (jiǎng) means prize, reward; to award. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 大 (big), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jiǎng.

  • prize
  • reward
  • to award

Decomposition

奖 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

奖 =
丬 top half of tree trunk
夕 bottom evening
大 part big

Radical

The radical of 奖 is 大 — meaning big. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 套奋失.

How the character is built

奖 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 大 (big) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 丬 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 奖

获奖 huòjiǎng to win a prize; to be awarded
他的作品获奖了。
Tā de zuòpǐn huòjiǎng le.
His work won an award.
奖 jiǎng award; prize
他得了一个大奖。
Tā dé le yí gè dà jiǎng.
He won a big prize.
奖金 jiǎngjīn bonus; cash prize
公司发了年终奖金。
Gōngsī fā le niánzhōng jiǎngjīn.
The company issued year-end bonuses.
奖学金 jiǎngxuéjīn scholarship
她获得了奖学金。
Tā huòdé le jiǎngxuéjīn.
She received a scholarship.
