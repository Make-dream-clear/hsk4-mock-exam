How to write 熟 (shú) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

熟
shú well-cooked; ripe, mature; familiar with 15 strokes · Radical 灬 (fire) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

熟 (shú) means well-cooked; ripe, mature; familiar with. It is written in 15 strokes, with the radical 灬 (fire), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #72 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shú.

  • well-cooked
  • ripe
  • mature
  • familiar with

Decomposition

熟 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

熟 =
孰 top who? which? what? which one?
灬 bottom fire

Radical

The radical of 熟 is 灬 — meaning fire. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

熟 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 灬 (fire) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 孰 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 熟

熟 shú/shóu cooked; ripe; familiar
苹果还没有熟。
Píngguǒ hái méiyǒu shú.
The apples aren't ripe yet.
熟悉 shúxi to be familiar with; to know well
他对北京的路很熟悉。
Tā duì Běijīng de lù hěn shúxi.
He is very familiar with Beijing's roads.
