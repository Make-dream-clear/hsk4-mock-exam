杂 (zá) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

杂
zá mix, blend; various, miscellaneous 6 strokes · Radical 木 (tree) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 杂 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

杂 (zá) means mix, blend; various, miscellaneous. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 木 (tree), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zá.

  • mix
  • blend
  • various
  • miscellaneous

Radical

The radical of 杂 is 木 — meaning tree. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 标村术案棒材格棵林梦桥染.
