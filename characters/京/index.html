How to write 京 (jīng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

京
jīng capital city 8 strokes · Radical 亠 (lid) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

京 (jīng) means capital city. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 亠 (lid), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #120 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jīng.

Decomposition

京 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Top + Middle + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

京 =
亠 top lid
口 middle mouth
小 bottom small

Radical

The radical of 京 is 亠 — meaning lid. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 产.

How the character is built

京 is a pictograph — a stylized image of the thing it names. It originally depicted A capital building on a hill.

HSK 4 Words Containing 京

京剧 jīngjù Peking Opera; Beijing Opera
京剧是中国的国粹。
Jīngjù shì Zhōngguó de guócuì.
Peking Opera is a national treasure of China.
