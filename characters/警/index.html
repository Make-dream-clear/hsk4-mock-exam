警 (jǐng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

警
jǐng to guard, to watch; alarm, alert 19 strokes · Radical 言 (words) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 警 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

警 (jǐng) means to guard, to watch; alarm, alert. It is written in 19 strokes, with the radical 言 (words), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jǐng.

  • to guard
  • to watch
  • alarm
  • alert

Decomposition

警 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

警 =
敬 top to respect
言 bottom words

Radical

The radical of 警 is 言 — meaning words. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

警 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 言 (speech) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 敬 originally indicated the sound.
