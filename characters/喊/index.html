How to write 喊 (hǎn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

喊
hǎn to shout, to yell, to call out; to howl; to cry 12 strokes · Radical 口 (mouth) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

喊 (hǎn) means to shout, to yell, to call out; to howl; to cry. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 口 (mouth), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #150 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced hǎn.

  • to shout
  • to yell
  • to call out
  • to howl
  • to cry

Decomposition

喊 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

喊 =
口 left mouth
咸 right all

Radical

The radical of 喊 is 口 — meaning mouth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 品吹台否另各味咳呼命嗯售.

How the character is built

喊 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 口 (mouth) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 咸 originally indicated the sound.
