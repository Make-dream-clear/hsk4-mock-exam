谅 (liàng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

谅
liàng to excuse, to forgive; to guess, to presume 10 strokes · Radical 讠 (words) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 谅 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

谅 (liàng) means to excuse, to forgive; to guess, to presume. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 讠 (words), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced liàng.

  • to excuse
  • to forgive
  • to guess
  • to presume

Decomposition

谅 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

谅 =
讠 left words
京 right capital city

Radical

The radical of 谅 is 讠 — meaning words. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 误计许谈讨论诚订评详译谊.

How the character is built

谅 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 讠 (speech) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 京 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 谅

Please forgive my mistake.
