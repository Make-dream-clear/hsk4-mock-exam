How to write 产 (chǎn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

产
chǎn to give birth, to bring forth, to produce 6 strokes · Radical 亠 (lid) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

产 (chǎn) means to give birth, to bring forth, to produce. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 亠 (lid), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #107 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced chǎn.

  • to give birth
  • to bring forth
  • to produce

Radical

The radical of 产 is 亠 — meaning lid. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 京.
