察 (chá) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

察
chá to examine, to investigate, to observe 14 strokes · Radical 宀 (roof) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 察 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

察 (chá) means to examine, to investigate, to observe. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 宀 (roof), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced chá.

  • to examine
  • to investigate
  • to observe

Decomposition

察 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

察 =
宀 top roof
祭 bottom to sacrifice to

Radical

The radical of 察 is 宀 — meaning roof. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 寄富寒密.

How the character is built

察 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 宀 (roof) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 祭 originally indicated the sound.
