祝 (zhù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

祝
zhù to pray; to wish well; surname 9 strokes · Radical 礻 (spirit) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 祝 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

祝 (zhù) means to pray; to wish well; surname. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 礻 (spirit), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhù.

  • to pray
  • to wish well
  • surname

Decomposition

祝 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

祝 =
礻 left spirit
兄 right elder brother

Radical

The radical of 祝 is 礻 — meaning spirit. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 社福.

How the character is built

祝 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A person 兄 praying before an altar 礻.

HSK 4 Words Containing 祝

庆祝 qìngzhù to celebrate
我们一起庆祝他的生日。
Wǒmen yìqǐ qìngzhù tā de shēngrì.
Let's celebrate his birthday together.
祝 zhù to wish; to express good wishes

Happy birthday!
祝贺 zhùhè to congratulate; congratulations
祝贺你考上了大学！
Zhùhè nǐ kǎo shàng le dàxué!
Congratulations on getting into university!
