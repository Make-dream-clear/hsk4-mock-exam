尤 (yóu) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

尤
yóu especially, particularly 4 strokes · Radical 尢 (weak) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 尤 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

尤 (yóu) means especially, particularly. It is written in 4 strokes, with the radical 尢 (weak), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yóu.

  • especially
  • particularly

Radical

The radical of 尤 is 尢 — meaning weak. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

HSK 4 Words Containing 尤

尤其 yóuqí especially; particularly
他喜欢运动，尤其是游泳。
Tā xǐhuan yùndòng, yóuqí shì yóuyǒng.
