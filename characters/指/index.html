How to write 指 (zhǐ) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

指
zhǐ finger, toe; to point, to indicate 9 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

指 (zhǐ) means finger, toe; to point, to indicate. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #83 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhǐ.

  • finger
  • toe
  • to point
  • to indicate

Decomposition

指 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

指 =
扌 left hand
旨 right aim

Radical

The radical of 指 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔掉拉挂推排按播擦操.

How the character is built

指 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. To point 旨 by hand 扌; 旨 also provides the pronunciation.

HSK 4 Words Containing 指

指 zhǐ to point to; to refer to
他指着地图说我们应该往东走。
Tā zhǐ zhe dìtú shuō wǒmen yīnggāi wǎng dōng zǒu.
He pointed at the map and said we should go east.
指出 zhǐchū to point out
老师指出了我作文中的问题。
Lǎoshī zhǐchū le wǒ zuòwén zhōng de wèntí.
The teacher pointed out the problems in my essay.
