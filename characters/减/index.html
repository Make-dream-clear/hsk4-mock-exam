减 (jiǎn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

减
jiǎn to decrease, to subtract, to diminish 11 strokes · Radical 冫 (ice) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 减 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

减 (jiǎn) means to decrease, to subtract, to diminish. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 冫 (ice), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jiǎn.

  • to decrease
  • to subtract
  • to diminish

Decomposition

减 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

减 =
冫 left ice
咸 right all

Radical

The radical of 减 is 冫 — meaning ice. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 况.

How the character is built

减 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 冫 (ice) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 咸 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 减

减 jiǎn to reduce; to subtract
你应该减少吃甜食。
Nǐ yīnggāi jiǎnshǎo chī tiánshí.
You should eat less sweets.
减轻 jiǎnqīng to lighten; to reduce (burden)
运动可以减轻压力。
Yùndòng kěyǐ jiǎnqīng yālì.
Exercise can reduce stress.
减少 jiǎnshǎo to reduce; to decrease
城市应该减少污染。
Chéngshì yīnggāi jiǎnshǎo wūrǎn.
Cities should reduce pollution.
