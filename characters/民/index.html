How to write 民 (mín) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

民
mín citizens, subjects; a nation's people 5 strokes · Radical 民 (citizens) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

民 (mín) means citizens, subjects; a nation's people. It is written in 5 strokes, with the radical 民 (citizens), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #146 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced mín.

  • citizens
  • subjects
  • a nation's people

Decomposition

民 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Overlap. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

民 =
巳 back the hours from 9 to 11
戈 front spear

Radical

The radical of 民 is 民 — meaning citizens. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

民 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. An eye 巳 pierced by a dagger 戈, an old mark of slavery.
