How to write 金 (jīn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

金
jīn gold, metal; money 8 strokes · Radical 金 (gold) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

金 (jīn) means gold, metal; money. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 金 (gold), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #38 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jīn.

  • gold
  • metal
  • money

Decomposition

金 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

金 =
人 top man
王 bottom king

Radical

The radical of 金 is 金 — meaning gold. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

金 is a pictograph — a stylized image of the thing it names. It originally depicted A cast-iron bell.

HSK 4 Words Containing 金

奖金 jiǎngjīn bonus; cash prize
公司发了年终奖金。
Gōngsī fā le niánzhōng jiǎngjīn.
The company issued year-end bonuses.
奖学金 jiǎngxuéjīn scholarship
她获得了奖学金。
Tā huòdé le jiǎngxuéjīn.
She received a scholarship.
现金 xiànjīn cash
对不起，我们不收现金。
Duìbuqǐ, wǒmen bù shōu xiànjīn.
Sorry, we don't accept cash.
