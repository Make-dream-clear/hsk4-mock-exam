桶 (tǒng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

桶
tǒng bucket, pail, tub; can, cask, keg 11 strokes · Radical 木 (tree) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 桶 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

桶 (tǒng) means bucket, pail, tub; can, cask, keg. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 木 (tree), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tǒng.

  • bucket
  • pail
  • tub
  • can
  • cask
  • keg

Decomposition

桶 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

桶 =
木 left tree
甬 right path

Radical

The radical of 桶 is 木 — meaning tree. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 标村术案棒材格棵林梦桥染.

How the character is built

桶 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 木 (wood) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 甬 originally indicated the sound.
