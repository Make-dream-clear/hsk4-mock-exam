嗯 (en) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

嗯
en interjection indicating agreement or appreciation 13 strokes · Radical 口 (mouth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 嗯 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

嗯 (en) means interjection indicating agreement or appreciation. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical 口 (mouth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Decomposition

嗯 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

嗯 =
口 left mouth
恩 right kindness

Radical

The radical of 嗯 is 口 — meaning mouth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 品吹台否另各味喊咳呼命售.

How the character is built

嗯 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 口 (mouth) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 恩 originally indicated the sound.
