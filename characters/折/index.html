折 (zhé) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

折
zhé to break, to snap; to fold, to bend; to bow; humble 7 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 折 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

折 (zhé) means to break, to snap; to fold, to bend; to bow; humble. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhé.

  • to break
  • to snap
  • to fold
  • to bend
  • to bow
  • humble

Decomposition

折 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

折 =
扌 left hand
斤 right a catty (about 500 grams)

Radical

The radical of 折 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔掉拉指挂推排按播擦.

How the character is built

折 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A hand 扌 wielding an axe 斤.
