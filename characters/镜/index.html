镜 (jìng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

镜
jìng mirror, glass; lens, glasses 16 strokes · Radical 钅 (gold) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 镜 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

镜 (jìng) means mirror, glass; lens, glasses. It is written in 16 strokes, with the radical 钅 (gold), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jìng.

  • mirror
  • glass
  • lens
  • glasses

Decomposition

镜 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

镜 =
钅 left gold
竟 right finally

Radical

The radical of 镜 is 钅 — meaning gold. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 钢键钥针.

How the character is built

镜 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 钅 (metal) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 竟 originally indicated the sound.
