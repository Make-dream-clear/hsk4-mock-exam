How to write 增 (zēng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

增
zēng to increase; to expand; to augment; to add 15 strokes · Radical 土 (soil) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

增 (zēng) means to increase; to expand; to augment; to add. It is written in 15 strokes, with the radical 土 (soil), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #75 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zēng.

  • to increase
  • to expand
  • to augment
  • to add

Decomposition

增 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

增 = 土 left soil
曾 right already

Radical

The radical of 增 is 土 — meaning soil. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 基堂土堵圾垃塑填址.

How the character is built

增 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 土 (earth) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 曾 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 增

增加 zēngjiā to increase; to add
公司计划增加五十名员工。
Gōngsī jìhuà zēngjiā wǔshí míng yuángōng.
The company plans to add fifty employees.
增长 zēngzhǎng to grow; to increase
今年的出口额增长了百分之十。
Jīnnián de chūkǒu'é zēngzhǎng le bǎi fēn zhī shí.
This year's exports grew by ten percent.
