译 (yì) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 译 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

译 (yì) means to translate, to interpret; to encode, to decode. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 讠 (words), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yì.

  • to translate
  • to interpret
  • to encode
  • to decode

Radical

The radical of 译 is 讠 — meaning words. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.
