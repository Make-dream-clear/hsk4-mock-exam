How to write 志 (zhì) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

志
zhì determination, will; mark, sign; to record, to write 7 strokes · Radical 心 (heart) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

志 (zhì) means determination, will; mark, sign; to record, to write. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 心 (heart), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #140 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhì.

  • determination
  • will
  • mark
  • sign
  • to record
  • to write

Decomposition

志 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

志 =
士 top scholar
心 bottom heart

Radical

The radical of 志 is 心 — meaning heart. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 忽态恐虑悉.

How the character is built

志 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. The mind 心 of a scholar 士; 士 also provides the pronunciation.
