释 (shì) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

释
shì to explain, to interpret; to release 12 strokes · Radical 釆 (to distinguish) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 释 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

释 (shì) means to explain, to interpret; to release. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 釆 (to distinguish), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shì.

  • to explain
  • to interpret
  • to release

Radical

The radical of 释 is 釆 — meaning to distinguish. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

释 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 釆 (collect) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 睪 originally indicated the sound.
