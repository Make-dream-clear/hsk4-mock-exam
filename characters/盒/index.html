盒 (hé) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

盒
hé small box or case; casket 11 strokes · Radical 皿 (dish) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 盒 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

盒 (hé) means small box or case; casket. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 皿 (dish), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced hé.

  • small box or case
  • casket

Decomposition

盒 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

盒 =
合 top to combine
皿 bottom dish

Radical

The radical of 盒 is 皿 — meaning dish. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 盐.

How the character is built

盒 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 皿 (container) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 合 originally indicated the sound.
