命 (mìng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

命
mìng life; destiny, fate, luck; an order, instruction 8 strokes · Radical 口 (mouth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 命 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

命 (mìng) means life; destiny, fate, luck; an order, instruction. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 口 (mouth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced mìng.

  • life
  • destiny
  • fate
  • luck
  • an order
  • instruction

Decomposition

命 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-right envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

命 =
令 outer command
口 inner mouth

Radical

The radical of 命 is 口 — meaning mouth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 品吹台否另各味喊咳呼嗯售.

How the character is built

命 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. An order 令 given by mouth 口.
