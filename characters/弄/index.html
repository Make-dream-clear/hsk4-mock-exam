How to write 弄 (nòng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

弄
nòng to do; to mock, to tease; to play with 7 strokes · Radical 廾 (two hands) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

弄 (nòng) means to do; to mock, to tease; to play with. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 廾 (two hands), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #149 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced nòng.

  • to do
  • to mock
  • to tease
  • to play with

Decomposition

弄 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

弄 =
玉 top jade
廾 bottom two hands

Radical

The radical of 弄 is 廾 — meaning two hands. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 弃.

How the character is built

弄 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Two hands 廾 playing with a piece of jade 玉; 廾 also provides the pronunciation.
