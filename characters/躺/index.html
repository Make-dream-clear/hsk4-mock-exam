躺 (tǎng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

躺
tǎng to recline, to lie down 15 strokes · Radical 身 (body) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 躺 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

躺 (tǎng) means to recline, to lie down. It is written in 15 strokes, with the radical 身 (body), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tǎng.

  • to recline
  • to lie down

Decomposition

躺 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

躺 =
身 left body
尚 right still

Radical

The radical of 躺 is 身 — meaning body. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

躺 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 身 (body) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 尚 originally indicated the sound.
