础 (chǔ) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

础
chǔ foundation stone, plinth; basis 10 strokes · Radical 石 (stone) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 础 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

础 (chǔ) means foundation stone, plinth; basis. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 石 (stone), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced chǔ.

  • foundation stone
  • plinth
  • basis

Decomposition

础 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

础 =
石 left stone
出 right to go out

Radical

The radical of 础 is 石 — meaning stone. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 确研破硕.

How the character is built

础 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 石 (stone) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 出 originally indicated the sound.
