骄 (jiāo) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

骄
jiāo haughty, spirited; a wild stallion 9 strokes · Radical 马 (horse) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 骄 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

骄 (jiāo) means haughty, spirited; a wild stallion. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 马 (horse), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jiāo.

  • haughty
  • spirited
  • a wild stallion

Decomposition

骄 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

骄 =
马 left horse
乔 right tall

Radical

The radical of 骄 is 马 — meaning horse. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 验.

How the character is built

骄 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A proud 乔 horse 马; 乔 also provides the pronunciation.

HSK 4 Words Containing 骄

骄傲 jiāo’ào arrogant; proud

Tā wèi zìjǐ de chéngjì gǎndào jiāo'ào.
She felt proud of her achievements.
