帅 (shuài) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

帅
shuài commander; commander-in-chief; handsome, smart 5 strokes · Radical 巾 (cloth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 帅 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

帅 (shuài) means commander; commander-in-chief; handsome, smart. It is written in 5 strokes, with the radical 巾 (cloth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shuài.

  • commander
  • commander-in-chief
  • handsome
  • smart

Decomposition

帅 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

帅 =
刂 left knife
巾 right cloth

Radical

The radical of 帅 is 巾 — meaning cloth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 幅巾帽.

How the character is built

帅 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. An army officer carrying a weapon 刂 and a flag 巾.
