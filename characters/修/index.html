修 (xiū) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

修
xiū to study; to repair; to decorate; to cultivate 9 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 修 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

修 (xiū) means to study; to repair; to decorate; to cultivate. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xiū.

  • to study
  • to repair
  • to decorate
  • to cultivate

Decomposition

修 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

修 =
攸 top distant
彡 bottom hair

Radical

The radical of 修 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 保任使倒低例俩份停傲倍付.

How the character is built

修 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 彡 (hair) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 攸 originally indicated the sound.
