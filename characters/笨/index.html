笨 (bèn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

笨
bèn stupid, foolish, dull, awkward 11 strokes · Radical ⺮ (bamboo) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 笨 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

笨 (bèn) means stupid, foolish, dull, awkward. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical ⺮ (bamboo), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bèn.

  • stupid
  • foolish
  • dull
  • awkward

Decomposition

笨 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

笨 =
⺮ top bamboo
本 bottom root

Radical

The radical of 笨 is ⺮ — meaning bamboo. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 篇管符籍签.

How the character is built

笨 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component ⺮ (bamboo) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 本 originally indicated the sound.
