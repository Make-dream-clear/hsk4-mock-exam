厨 (chú) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

厨
chú kitchen; closet, cupboard 12 strokes · Radical 厂 (cliff) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 厨 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

厨 (chú) means kitchen; closet, cupboard. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 厂 (cliff), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced chú.

  • kitchen
  • closet
  • cupboard

Decomposition

厨 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

厨 = 厂 outer cliff
豆 inner beans
寸 part inch

Radical

The radical of 厨 is 厂 — meaning cliff. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 压厂原厕厚厉厅厌质.

How the character is built

厨 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 厂 (building) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 豆 originally indicated the sound.
