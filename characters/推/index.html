How to write 推 (tuī) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

推
tuī to push; to expel; to drive; to decline 11 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

推 (tuī) means to push; to expel; to drive; to decline. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #53 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tuī.

  • to push
  • to expel
  • to drive
  • to decline

Decomposition

推 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

推 =
扌 left hand
隹 right short-tailed bird

Radical

The radical of 推 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔掉拉指挂排按播擦操.

How the character is built

推 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 隹 originally indicated the sound.
