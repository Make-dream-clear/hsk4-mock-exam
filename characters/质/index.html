质 (zhì) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

质
zhì essence, nature; material, substance 8 strokes · Radical 厂 (cliff) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 质 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

质 (zhì) means essence, nature; material, substance. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 厂 (cliff), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhì.

  • essence
  • nature
  • material
  • substance

Decomposition

质 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

质 = 厂 outer cliff
十 inner ten
贝 part sea shell

Radical

The radical of 质 is 厂 — meaning cliff. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 压厂原厕厨厚厉厅厌.

How the character is built

质 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 贝 (shell) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 十 originally indicated the sound.
