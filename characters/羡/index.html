羡 (xiàn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

羡
xiàn to envy, to covet; to admire, to praise 12 strokes · Radical 羊 (sheep) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 羡 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

羡 (xiàn) means to envy, to covet; to admire, to praise. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 羊 (sheep), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xiàn.

  • to envy
  • to covet
  • to admire
  • to praise

Decomposition

羡 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

羡 =
羊 top sheep
次 bottom order

Radical

The radical of 羡 is 羊 — meaning sheep. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 美羞.

How the character is built

羡 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Someone who has a whole row 次 of sheep 羊.
