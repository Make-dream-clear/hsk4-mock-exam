How to write 济 (jì) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

济
jì to aid, to help, to relieve; to ferry across 9 strokes · Radical 氵 (water) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

济 (jì) means to aid, to help, to relieve; to ferry across. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 氵 (water), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #108 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jì.

  • to aid
  • to help
  • to relieve
  • to ferry across

Decomposition

济 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

济 =
氵 left water
齐 right even

Radical

The radical of 济 is 氵 — meaning water. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 江深油汤流温汗浪漫泼污消.

How the character is built

济 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 氵 (water) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 齐 originally indicated the sound.
