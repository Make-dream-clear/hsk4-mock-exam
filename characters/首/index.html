首 (shǒu) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

首
shǒu chief, head, leader 9 strokes · Radical 首 (chief) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 首 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

首 (shǒu) means chief, head, leader. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 首 (chief), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shǒu.

  • chief
  • head
  • leader

Decomposition

首 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

首 =
丷 top kwukyel
自 bottom self

Radical

The radical of 首 is 首 — meaning chief. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

首 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A deer's head 自 adorned with antlers 丷.

HSK 4 Words Containing 首

首 shǒu (measure word: songs, poems)
请你唱一首歌吧。
Qǐng nǐ chàng yì shǒu gē ba.
Please sing a song.
首都 shǒudū capital city
北京是中国的首都。
Běijīng shì Zhōngguó de shǒudū.
Beijing is the capital of China.
首先 shǒuxiān first; first of all
首先，我想感谢大家。
Shǒuxiān, wǒ xiǎng gǎnxiè dàjiā.
First of all, I want to thank everyone.
