稍 (shāo) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

稍
shāo somewhat, slightly, a little; rather 12 strokes · Radical 禾 (cereal) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 稍 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

稍 (shāo) means somewhat, slightly, a little; rather. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 禾 (cereal), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shāo.

  • somewhat
  • slightly
  • a little
  • rather

Decomposition

稍 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

稍 =
禾 left cereal
肖 right to resemble

Radical

The radical of 稍 is 禾 — meaning cereal. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 程科积秒秀.

How the character is built

稍 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Resembling 肖 grain 禾; 肖 also provides the pronunciation.
