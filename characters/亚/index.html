How to write 亚 (yà) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

亚
yà Asia; second 6 strokes · Radical 一 (one) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

亚 (yà) means Asia; second. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 一 (one), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #111 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yà.

  • Asia
  • second

Decomposition

亚 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

亚 =
一 top one
业 bottom business

Radical

The radical of 亚 is 一 — meaning one. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 与专严.

How the character is built

亚 is a pictograph — a stylized image of the thing it names. It originally depicted Picture of a cross-shaped house or temple.
