烟 (yān) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

烟
yān smoke, soot; opium; tobacco, cigarettes 10 strokes · Radical 火 (fire) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 烟 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

烟 (yān) means smoke, soot; opium; tobacco, cigarettes. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 火 (fire), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yān.

  • smoke
  • soot
  • opium
  • tobacco
  • cigarettes

Decomposition

烟 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

烟 =
火 left fire
因 right cause

Radical

The radical of 烟 is 火 — meaning fire. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 烦烤.

How the character is built

烟 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 火 (fire) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 因 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 烟

烟 yān cigarette; smoke
吸烟有害健康。
Xī yān yǒuhài jiànkāng.
Smoking is harmful to health.
