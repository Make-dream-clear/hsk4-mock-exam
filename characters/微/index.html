微 (wēi) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

微
wēi small, tiny, trifling; micro- 13 strokes · Radical 彳 (to step with the left foot) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 微 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

微 (wēi) means small, tiny, trifling; micro-. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical 彳 (to step with the left foot), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced wēi.

  • small
  • tiny
  • trifling
  • micro-

Radical

The radical of 微 is 彳 — meaning to step with the left foot. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 待律.

HSK 4 Words Containing 微

稍微 shāowēi slightly; a bit
今天稍微有点冷。
Jīntiān shāowēi yǒudiǎn lěng.
It's a little bit cold today.
