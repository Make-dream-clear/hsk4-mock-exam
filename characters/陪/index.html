陪 (péi) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

陪
péi to accompany, to be with, to keep company 10 strokes · Radical 阝 (place) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 陪 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

陪 (péi) means to accompany, to be with, to keep company. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 阝 (place), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced péi.

  • to accompany
  • to be with
  • to keep company

Decomposition

陪 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

陪 =
阝 left place
咅 right to spit out

Radical

The radical of 陪 is 阝 — meaning place. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 部际随队降郊险.

How the character is built

陪 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 阝 (place) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 咅 originally indicated the sound.
