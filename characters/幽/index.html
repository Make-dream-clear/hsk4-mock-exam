幽 (yōu) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

幽
yōu quiet, secluded, tranquil; dark 9 strokes · Radical 幺 (one) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 幽 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

幽 (yōu) means quiet, secluded, tranquil; dark. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 幺 (one), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yōu.

  • quiet
  • secluded
  • tranquil
  • dark

Radical

The radical of 幽 is 幺 — meaning one. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 乡.

How the character is built

幽 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Small niches 幺 in the mountainside 山; 幺 also provides the pronunciation.
