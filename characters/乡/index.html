乡 (xiāng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

乡
xiāng country, village; rural 3 strokes · Radical 幺 (one) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 乡 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

乡 (xiāng) means country, village; rural. It is written in 3 strokes, with the radical 幺 (one), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xiāng.

  • country
  • village
  • rural

Radical

The radical of 乡 is 幺 — meaning one. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 幽.
