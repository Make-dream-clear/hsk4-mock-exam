寒 (hán) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

寒
hán chilly, cold; poor; to shiver, to tremble 12 strokes · Radical 宀 (roof) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 寒 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

寒 (hán) means chilly, cold; poor; to shiver, to tremble. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 宀 (roof), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced hán.

  • chilly
  • cold
  • poor
  • to shiver
  • to tremble

Radical

The radical of 寒 is 宀 — meaning roof. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 寄察富密.
