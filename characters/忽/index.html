How to write 忽 (hū) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

忽
hū suddenly, abruptly; to neglect 8 strokes · Radical 心 (heart) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

忽 (hū) means suddenly, abruptly; to neglect. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 心 (heart), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #99 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced hū.

  • suddenly
  • abruptly
  • to neglect

Decomposition

忽 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

忽 =
勿 top must not
心 bottom heart

Radical

The radical of 忽 is 心 — meaning heart. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 态志恐虑悉.

How the character is built

忽 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 心 (heart) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 勿 originally indicated the sound.

Hūrán xià qǐ le dà yǔ.
It suddenly started raining heavily.
