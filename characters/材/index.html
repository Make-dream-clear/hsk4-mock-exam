材 (cái) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

材
cái timber; material, stuff; talent 7 strokes · Radical 木 (tree) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 材 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

材 (cái) means timber; material, stuff; talent. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 木 (tree), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced cái.

  • timber
  • material
  • stuff
  • talent

Decomposition

材 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

材 =
木 left tree
才 right ability

Radical

The radical of 材 is 木 — meaning tree. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 标村术案棒格棵林梦桥染森.

How the character is built

材 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 木 (wood) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 才 originally indicated the sound.
