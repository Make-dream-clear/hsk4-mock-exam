辣 (là) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

辣
là pepper; hot, spicy; cruel 14 strokes · Radical 辛 (bitter) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 辣 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

辣 (là) means pepper; hot, spicy; cruel. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 辛 (bitter), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced là.

  • pepper
  • hot
  • spicy
  • cruel

Decomposition

辣 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

辣 = 辛 left bitter
束 right to bind

Radical

The radical of 辣 is 辛 — meaning bitter. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 辛.

How the character is built

辣 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 辛 (bitter) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 束 originally indicated the sound.
