博 (bó) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

博
bó to gamble, to play games; to win; rich, plentiful, extensive; wide, broad 12 strokes · Radical 十 (ten) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 博 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

博 (bó) means to gamble, to play games; to win; rich, plentiful, extensive; wide, broad. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 十 (ten), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bó.

  • to gamble
  • to play games
  • to win
  • rich
  • plentiful
  • extensive

Decomposition

博 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

博 =
十 left ten
尃 right to announce

Radical

The radical of 博 is 十 — meaning ten. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

博 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 十 (ten) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 尃 originally indicated the sound.
