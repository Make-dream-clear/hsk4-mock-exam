项 (xiàng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

项
xiàng neck, nape; item; a term in an equation 9 strokes · Radical 页 (page) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 项 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

项 (xiàng) means neck, nape; item; a term in an equation. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 页 (page), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xiàng.

  • neck
  • nape
  • item
  • a term in an equation

Decomposition

项 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

项 =
工 left labor
页 right page

Radical

The radical of 项 is 页 — meaning page. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 顺顿频预.

How the character is built

项 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 页 (head) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 工 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 项

项 xiàng (measure word: items, matters)
他一共完成了三项任务。
Tā yígòng wánchéng le sān xiàng rènwu.
He completed a total of three tasks.
