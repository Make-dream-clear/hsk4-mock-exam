死 (sǐ) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

死
sǐ dead; death; impassable, inflexible 6 strokes · Radical 歹 (corpse) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 死 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

死 (sǐ) means dead; death; impassable, inflexible. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 歹 (corpse), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced sǐ.

  • dead
  • death
  • impassable
  • inflexible

Decomposition

死 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

死 =
歹 outer corpse
匕 inner spoon

Radical

The radical of 死 is 歹 — meaning corpse. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

死 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A person kneeling 匕 before a corpse 歹.

HSK 4 Words Containing 死

死 sǐ to die; dead; extremely (informal)
今天热死了！
Jīntiān rè sǐ le!
