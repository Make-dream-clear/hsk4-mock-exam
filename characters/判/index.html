判 (pàn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

判
pàn to judge; to discriminate; to conclude 7 strokes · Radical 刂 (knife) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 判 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

判 (pàn) means to judge; to discriminate; to conclude. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 刂 (knife), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced pàn.

  • to judge
  • to discriminate
  • to conclude

Decomposition

判 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

判 =
半 left half
刂 right knife

Radical

The radical of 判 is 刂 — meaning knife. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 划利剧列剩则.

How the character is built

判 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 刂 (knife) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 半 originally indicated the sound.
