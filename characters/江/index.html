How to write 江 (jiāng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

江
jiāng large river; the Yangtze; surname 6 strokes · Radical 氵 (water) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

江 (jiāng) means large river; the Yangtze; surname. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 氵 (water), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #114 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jiāng.

  • large river
  • the Yangtze
  • surname

Decomposition

江 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

江 =
氵 left water
工 right labor

Radical

The radical of 江 is 氵 — meaning water. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 济深油汤流温汗浪漫泼污消.

How the character is built

江 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 氵 (water) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 工 originally indicated the sound.
