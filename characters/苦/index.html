苦 (kǔ) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

苦
kǔ bitter; hardship, suffering 8 strokes · Radical 艹 (grass) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 苦 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

苦 (kǔ) means bitter; hardship, suffering. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 艹 (grass), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced kǔ.

  • bitter
  • hardship
  • suffering

Decomposition

苦 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

苦 =
艹 top grass
古 bottom old

Radical

The radical of 苦 is 艹 — meaning grass. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 获落葡萄艺著.

How the character is built

苦 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 艹 (grass) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 古 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 苦

苦 kǔ bitter
这杯咖啡太苦了。
Zhè bēi kāfēi tài kǔ le.
This coffee is too bitter.
辛苦 xīnkǔ hard; tiring; laborious
老师，您辛苦了！
Lǎoshī, nín xīnkǔ le!
Teacher, you've worked hard!
