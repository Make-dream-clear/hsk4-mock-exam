How to write 原 (yuán) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

原
yuán source, origin, beginning 10 strokes · Radical 厂 (cliff) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

原 (yuán) means source, origin, beginning. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 厂 (cliff), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #43 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yuán.

  • source
  • origin
  • beginning

Decomposition

原 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

原 = 厂 outer cliff
泉 inner spring

Radical

The radical of 原 is 厂 — meaning cliff. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 压厂厕厨厚厉厅厌质.

How the character is built

原 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 厂 (cliff) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 泉 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 原

原来 yuánlái original; former; originally; it turns out
原来是你啊！
Yuánlái shì nǐ a!
Oh, it's you! (So it turns out it was you!)
原谅 yuánliàng to forgive; to excuse
请你原谅我的错误。
Qǐng nǐ yuánliàng wǒ de cuòwù.
Please forgive my mistake.
原因 yuányīn reason; cause
迟到的原因是堵车了。
Chídào de yuányīn shì dǔchē le.
The reason for being late was traffic.
