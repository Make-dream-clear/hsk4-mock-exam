键 (jiàn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

键
jiàn lock, door bolt; key 13 strokes · Radical 钅 (gold) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 键 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

键 (jiàn) means lock, door bolt; key. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical 钅 (gold), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jiàn.

  • lock
  • door bolt
  • key

Decomposition

键 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

键 =
钅 left gold
建 right to build

Radical

The radical of 键 is 钅 — meaning gold. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 钢镜钥针.

How the character is built

键 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 钅 (metal) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 建 originally indicated the sound.
