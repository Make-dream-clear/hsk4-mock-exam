戚 (qī) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

戚
qī ashamed, grieving; related to; relative 11 strokes · Radical 戈 (spear) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 戚 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

戚 (qī) means ashamed, grieving; related to; relative. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 戈 (spear), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced qī.

  • ashamed
  • grieving
  • related to
  • relative

Decomposition

戚 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Outer (open below) + Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

戚 =
戊 outer 5th heavenly stem
尗 inner younger brother

Radical

The radical of 戚 is 戈 — meaning spear. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 戴.

How the character is built

戚 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A younger brother 尗 killed in a war 戈.
