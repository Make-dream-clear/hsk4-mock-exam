How to write 背 (bèi) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

背
bèi back, backside; to betray, to violate 9 strokes · Radical ⺼ (meat) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

背 (bèi) means back, backside; to betray, to violate. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical ⺼ (meat), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #58 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bèi.

  • back
  • backside
  • to betray
  • to violate

Decomposition

背 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

背 =
北 top north
⺼ bottom meat

Radical

The radical of 背 is ⺼ — meaning meat. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 膊肚肤膏胳肯脾脱.

How the character is built

背 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component ⺼ (flesh) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 北 originally indicated the sound.
