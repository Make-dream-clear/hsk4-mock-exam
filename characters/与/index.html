How to write 与 (yǔ) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

与
yǔ and; with; to; for; to give, to grant 3 strokes · Radical 一 (one) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

与 (yǔ) means and; with; to; for; to give, to grant. It is written in 3 strokes, with the radical 一 (one), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #126 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yǔ.

  • and
  • with
  • to
  • for
  • to give
  • to grant

Radical

The radical of 与 is 一 — meaning one. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 亚专严.
