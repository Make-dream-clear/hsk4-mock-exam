匙 (chí / shī) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

匙
chí / shī spoon; surname 11 strokes · Radical 匕 (spoon) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 匙 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

匙 (chí / shī) means spoon; surname. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 匕 (spoon), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

This character has 2 readings: chíshī. The reading depends on which word the character appears in.

  • spoon
  • surname

Decomposition

匙 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Lower-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

匙 =
是 outer to be
匕 inner spoon

Radical

The radical of 匙 is 匕 — meaning spoon. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

匙 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 匕 (spoon) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 是 originally indicated the sound.
