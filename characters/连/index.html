How to write 连 (lián) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

连
lián to join, to connect; continuous; even 7 strokes · Radical 辶 (to walk) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

连 (lián) means to join, to connect; continuous; even. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 辶 (to walk), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #137 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced lián.

  • to join
  • to connect
  • continuous
  • even

Decomposition

连 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Lower-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

连 =
辶 outer to walk
车 inner cart

Radical

The radical of 连 is 辶 — meaning to walk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 达通递逛迷速邀.

How the character is built

连 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A cart 车 moving 辶 goods between cities.

HSK 4 Words Containing 连

连 lián even (prep); to connect; continuously
他连饭都没吃就走了。
Tā lián fàn dōu méi chī jiù zǒu le.
He left without even eating.
