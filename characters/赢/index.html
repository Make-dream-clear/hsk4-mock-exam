赢 (yíng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

赢
yíng to win, to gain; surplus, profit 17 strokes · Radical 贝 (sea shell) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 赢 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

赢 (yíng) means to win, to gain; surplus, profit. It is written in 17 strokes, with the radical 贝 (sea shell), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yíng.

  • to win
  • to gain
  • surplus
  • profit

Decomposition

赢 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

赢 =
吂 top
⺼ bottom meat
贝 part sea shell

Radical

The radical of 赢 is 贝 — meaning sea shell. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 资败费负购贺货责账赚.

HSK 4 Words Containing 赢

赢 yíng to win; to beat
最后我们赢了这场比赛。
Zuìhòu wǒmen yíng le zhè chǎng bǐsài.
We won the game in the end.
赢得 yíngdé to win; to gain
他赢得了大家的尊重。
Tā yíngdé le dàjiā de zūnzhòng.
He earned everyone's respect.
