惊 (jīng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

惊
jīng to frighten, to startle; surprise, alarm 11 strokes · Radical 忄 (heart) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 惊 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

惊 (jīng) means to frighten, to startle; surprise, alarm. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 忄 (heart), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jīng.

  • to frighten
  • to startle
  • surprise
  • alarm

Decomposition

惊 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

惊 =
忄 left heart
京 right capital city

Radical

The radical of 惊 is 忄 — meaning heart. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 性怀悔懒恼惜忆愉.

How the character is built

惊 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 忄 (heart) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 京 originally indicated the sound.
