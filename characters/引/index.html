引 (yǐn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

引
yǐn to pull, to stretch; to draw; to attract 4 strokes · Radical 弓 (bow) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 引 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

引 (yǐn) means to pull, to stretch; to draw; to attract. It is written in 4 strokes, with the radical 弓 (bow), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yǐn.

  • to pull
  • to stretch
  • to draw
  • to attract

Decomposition

引 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

引 =
弓 left bow
丨 right number one

Radical

The radical of 引 is 弓 — meaning bow. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 强弹.

How the character is built

引 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. To pull back a bow's 弓 drawstring 丨.

HSK 4 Words Containing 引

吸引 xīyǐn to attract; to draw
这个城市吸引了很多游客。
Zhège chéngshì xīyǐn le hěn duō yóukè.
This city attracts many tourists.
引起 yǐnqǐ to cause; to trigger; to arouse
这件事引起了大家的注意。
Zhè jiàn shì yǐnqǐ le dàjiā de zhùyì.
This matter attracted everyone's attention.
