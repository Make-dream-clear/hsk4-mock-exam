秀 (xiù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

秀
xiù elegant, graceful, refined; flowing, luxuriant 7 strokes · Radical 禾 (cereal) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 秀 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

秀 (xiù) means elegant, graceful, refined; flowing, luxuriant. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 禾 (cereal), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xiù.

  • elegant
  • graceful
  • refined
  • flowing
  • luxuriant

Radical

The radical of 秀 is 禾 — meaning cereal. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 程科积秒稍.
