饼 (bǐng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

饼
bǐng rice-cake, pastry, biscuit 9 strokes · Radical 饣 (food) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 饼 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

饼 (bǐng) means rice-cake, pastry, biscuit. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 饣 (food), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bǐng.

  • rice-cake
  • pastry
  • biscuit

Decomposition

饼 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

饼 =
饣 left food
并 right to combine

Radical

The radical of 饼 is 饣 — meaning food. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 馒.

How the character is built

饼 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 饣 (food) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 并 originally indicated the sound.
