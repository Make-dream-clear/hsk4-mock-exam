How to write 断 (duàn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

断
duàn to sever, to cut off, to interrupt 11 strokes · Radical 斤 (a catty (about 500 grams)) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

断 (duàn) means to sever, to cut off, to interrupt. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 斤 (a catty (about 500 grams)), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #76 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced duàn.

  • to sever
  • to cut off
  • to interrupt

Decomposition

断 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

断 =
米 left rice
斤 right a catty (about 500 grams)

Radical

The radical of 断 is 斤 — meaning a catty (about 500 grams). Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

断 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Using an axe 斤 to harvest grain 米.

HSK 4 Words Containing 断

不断 búduàn continuously; constantly
科技在不断发展。
Kējì zài búduàn fāzhǎn.
Technology is constantly developing.
判断 pànduàn to judge; to assess
你要自己判断对错。
Nǐ yào zìjǐ pànduàn duìcuò.
You need to judge right and wrong yourself.
