How to write 联 (lián) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

联
lián ally, associate; to connect, to join 12 strokes · Radical 耳 (ear) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

联 (lián) means ally, associate; to connect, to join. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 耳 (ear), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #82 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced lián.

  • ally
  • associate
  • to connect
  • to join

Decomposition

联 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

联 =
耳 left ear
关 right frontier pass

Radical

The radical of 联 is 耳 — meaning ear. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 聚聘职.

How the character is built

联 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 耳 (ear) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 关 originally indicated the sound.
