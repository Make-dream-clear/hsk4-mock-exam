How to write 油 (yóu) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

油
yóu oil, fat, grease, lard; oil paints 8 strokes · Radical 氵 (water) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

油 (yóu) means oil, fat, grease, lard; oil paints. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 氵 (water), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #40 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yóu.

  • oil
  • fat
  • grease
  • lard
  • oil paints

Decomposition

油 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

油 =
氵 left water
由 right cause

Radical

The radical of 油 is 氵 — meaning water. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 济江深汤流温汗浪漫泼污消.

How the character is built

油 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 氵 (water) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 由 originally indicated the sound.
