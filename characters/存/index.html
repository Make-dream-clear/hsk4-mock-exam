存 (cún) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

存
cún to exist; to survive, to maintain; to keep, to store; to deposit 6 strokes · Radical 子 (son) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 存 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

存 (cún) means to exist; to survive, to maintain; to keep, to store; to deposit. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 子 (son), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced cún.

  • to exist
  • to survive
  • to maintain
  • to keep
  • to store
  • to deposit

Decomposition

存 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

存 =
才 outer ability
子 inner son

Radical

The radical of 存 is 子 — meaning son. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 孙.

How the character is built

存 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 子 (seed) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 才 originally indicated the sound.
