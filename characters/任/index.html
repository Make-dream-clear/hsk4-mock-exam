How to write 任 (rèn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

任
rèn to trust, to rely on; to appoint; to bear; duty, office 6 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

任 (rèn) means to trust, to rely on; to appoint; to bear; duty, office. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #36 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced rèn.

  • to trust
  • to rely on
  • to appoint
  • to bear
  • duty
  • office

Decomposition

任 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

任 =
亻 left man
壬 right 9th heavenly stem

Radical

The radical of 任 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 保使倒低例俩份停傲倍付傅.

How the character is built

任 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 亻 (person) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 壬 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 任

任何 rènhé any; whatever
任何人都不能迟到。
Rènhé rén dōu bù néng chídào.
No one is allowed to be late.
任务 rènwu task; mission; assignment
他很快就完成了任务。
Tā hěn kuài jiù wánchéng le rènwu.
He completed the task very quickly.
责任 zérèn responsibility; duty
保护环境是每个人的责任。
Bǎohù huánjìng shì měi gè rén de zérèn.
Protecting the environment is everyone's responsibility.
