巾 (jīn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

巾
jīn cloth, curtain, handkerchief, towel 3 strokes · Radical 巾 (cloth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 巾 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

巾 (jīn) means cloth, curtain, handkerchief, towel. It is written in 3 strokes, with the radical 巾 (cloth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jīn.

  • cloth
  • curtain
  • handkerchief
  • towel

Radical

The radical of 巾 is 巾 — meaning cloth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 幅帽帅.

How the character is built

巾 is a pictograph — a stylized image of the thing it names. It originally depicted A curtain or towel hanging from a rack.
