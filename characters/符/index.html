符 (fú) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

符
fú amulet, charm; mark, tag; to correspond to 11 strokes · Radical ⺮ (bamboo) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 符 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

符 (fú) means amulet, charm; mark, tag; to correspond to. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical ⺮ (bamboo), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced fú.

  • amulet
  • charm
  • mark
  • tag
  • to correspond to

Decomposition

符 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

符 =
⺮ top bamboo
付 bottom give

Radical

The radical of 符 is ⺮ — meaning bamboo. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 篇管笨籍签.

How the character is built

符 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component ⺮ (bamboo) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 付 originally indicated the sound.
