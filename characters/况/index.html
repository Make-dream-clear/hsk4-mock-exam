How to write 况 (kuàng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

况
kuàng condition, situation; furthermore 7 strokes · Radical 冫 (ice) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

况 (kuàng) means condition, situation; furthermore. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 冫 (ice), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #129 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced kuàng.

  • condition
  • situation
  • furthermore

Decomposition

况 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

况 =
冫 left ice
兄 right elder brother

Radical

The radical of 况 is 冫 — meaning ice. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 减.

HSK 4 Words Containing 况

情况 qíngkuàng situation; condition; circumstances
请你介绍一下公司的情况。
Qǐng nǐ jièshào yíxià gōngsī de qíngkuàng.
Please introduce the situation of the company.
