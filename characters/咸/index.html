咸 (xián) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

咸
xián all, together, united; salted 9 strokes · Radical 口 (mouth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 咸 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

咸 (xián) means all, together, united; salted. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 口 (mouth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xián.

  • all
  • together
  • united
  • salted

Decomposition

咸 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Outer (open below) + Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

咸 =
戊 outer 5th heavenly stem
一 inner one
口 part mouth

Radical

The radical of 咸 is 口 — meaning mouth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 品吹台否另各味喊咳呼命嗯.

How the character is built

咸 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. One 一 voice 口 surrounded by conflict 戈.
