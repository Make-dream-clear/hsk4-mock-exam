How to write 麻 (má) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

麻
má hemp, flax, sesame; numb 11 strokes · Radical 麻 (hemp) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

麻 (má) means hemp, flax, sesame; numb. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 麻 (hemp), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #143 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced má.

  • hemp
  • flax
  • sesame
  • numb

Decomposition

麻 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

麻 = 广 outer broad 林 inner forest

Radical

The radical of 麻 is 麻 — meaning hemp. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

麻 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Hemp plants 林 growing in a greenhouse 广.
