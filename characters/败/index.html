败 (bài) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

败
bài failure; to decline, to fail, to suffer defeat 8 strokes · Radical 贝 (sea shell) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 败 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

败 (bài) means failure; to decline, to fail, to suffer defeat. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 贝 (sea shell), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bài.

  • failure
  • to decline
  • to fail
  • to suffer defeat

Decomposition

败 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

败 =
贝 left sea shell
攵 right to rap

Radical

The radical of 败 is 贝 — meaning sea shell. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 资费负购贺货赢责账赚.

How the character is built

败 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 攵 (rap) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 贝 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 败

打败 dǎbài to defeat; to beat
我们的球队打败了对手。
Wǒmen de qiúduì dǎbài le duìshǒu.
Our team defeated the opponent.

失败是成功之母。
Shībài shì chénggōng zhī mǔ.
Failure is the mother of success.
