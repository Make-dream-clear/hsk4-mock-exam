龄 (líng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

龄
líng age; years 13 strokes · Radical 齿 (teeth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 龄 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

龄 (líng) means age; years. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical 齿 (teeth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced líng.

  • age
  • years

Decomposition

龄 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

龄 =
齿 left teeth
令 right command

Radical

The radical of 龄 is 齿 — meaning teeth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

龄 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 齿 (age) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 令 originally indicated the sound.

You look younger than your actual age.
