围 (wéi) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

围
wéi to surround, to encircle, to corral 7 strokes · Radical 囗 (enclosure) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 围 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

围 (wéi) means to surround, to encircle, to corral. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 囗 (enclosure), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced wéi.

  • to surround
  • to encircle
  • to corral

Decomposition

围 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Outer surrounds Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

围 =
囗 outer enclosure
韦 inner tanned leather

Radical

The radical of 围 is 囗 — meaning enclosure. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 困.

How the character is built

围 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 囗 (enclosure) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 韦 originally indicated the sound.

Tā de zhōuwéi dōu shì péngyou.
