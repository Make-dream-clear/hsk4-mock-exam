How to write 装 (zhuāng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

装
zhuāng dress, clothes, attire; to wear, to install 12 strokes · Radical 衣 (cloth) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

装 (zhuāng) means dress, clothes, attire; to wear, to install. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 衣 (cloth), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #144 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhuāng.

  • dress
  • clothes
  • attire
  • to wear
  • to install

Decomposition

装 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

装 =
壮 top big
衣 bottom cloth

Radical

The radical of 装 is 衣 — meaning cloth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 袋.

How the character is built

装 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 衣 (clothes) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 壮 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 装

装 zhuāng to pretend; to pack; to install
她把衣服装进了行李箱。
Tā bǎ yīfu zhuāng jìn le xínglixiāng.
She packed her clothes into the suitcase.
