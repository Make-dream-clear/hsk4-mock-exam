厚 (hòu) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

厚
hòu generous; substantial; deep (as a friendship) 9 strokes · Radical 厂 (cliff) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 厚 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

厚 (hòu) means generous; substantial; deep (as a friendship). It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 厂 (cliff), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced hòu.

  • generous
  • substantial
  • deep (as a friendship)

Decomposition

厚 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

厚 = 厂 outer cliff
日 inner sun
子 part son

Radical

The radical of 厚 is 厂 — meaning cliff. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 压厂原厕厨厉厅厌质.

How the character is built

厚 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Sunlight 日 and children 子 in a building 厂.

HSK 4 Words Containing 厚

Dōngtiān yào chuān hòu yīfu.
You should wear thick clothes in winter.
