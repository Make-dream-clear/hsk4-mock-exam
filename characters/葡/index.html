葡 (pú) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

葡
pú grapes; Portugal, Portuguese 12 strokes · Radical 艹 (grass) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 葡 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

葡 (pú) means grapes; Portugal, Portuguese. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 艹 (grass), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced pú.

Decomposition

葡 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

葡 =
艹 top grass
匍 bottom to crawl

Radical

The radical of 葡 is 艹 — meaning grass. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 获苦落萄艺著.

How the character is built

葡 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 艹 (plant) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 匍 originally indicated the sound.
