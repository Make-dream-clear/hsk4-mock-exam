批 (pī) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

批
pī comment, criticism; batch, lot; wholesale 7 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 批 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

批 (pī) means comment, criticism; batch, lot; wholesale. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced pī.

  • comment
  • criticism
  • batch
  • lot
  • wholesale

Decomposition

批 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

批 =
扌 left hand
比 right to compare

Radical

The radical of 批 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔掉拉指挂推排按播擦.

How the character is built

批 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 比 originally indicated the sound.
