How to write 术 (shù) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

术
shù skill, art; method, technique; trick 5 strokes · Radical 木 (tree) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

术 (shù) means skill, art; method, technique; trick. It is written in 5 strokes, with the radical 木 (tree), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #74 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shù.

  • skill
  • art
  • method
  • technique
  • trick

Decomposition

术 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Lower-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

木 outer tree

Radical

The radical of 术 is 木 — meaning tree. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 标村案棒材格棵林梦桥染森.

HSK 4 Words Containing 术

技术 jìshù technology; technique; skill
他的电脑技术很好。
Tā de diànnǎo jìshù hěn hǎo.
He has great computer skills.
艺术 yìshù art
他对艺术非常感兴趣。
Tā duì yìshù fēicháng gǎn xìngqù.
He is very interested in art.
