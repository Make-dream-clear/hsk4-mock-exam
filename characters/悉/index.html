悉 (xī) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

悉
xī to know, to learn about, to comprehend 11 strokes · Radical 心 (heart) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 悉 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

悉 (xī) means to know, to learn about, to comprehend. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 心 (heart), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xī.

  • to know
  • to learn about
  • to comprehend

Decomposition

悉 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

悉 =
釆 top to distinguish
心 bottom heart

Radical

The radical of 悉 is 心 — meaning heart. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 忽态志恐虑.

How the character is built

悉 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 心 (mind) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 釆 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 悉

He is very familiar with Beijing's roads.
