弹 (dàn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

弹
dàn bullet, pellet, shell; elastic, springy 11 strokes · Radical 弓 (bow) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 弹 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

弹 (dàn) means bullet, pellet, shell; elastic, springy. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 弓 (bow), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced dàn.

  • bullet
  • pellet
  • shell
  • elastic
  • springy

Decomposition

弹 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

弹 =
弓 left bow
单 right single

Radical

The radical of 弹 is 弓 — meaning bow. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 强引.

How the character is built

弹 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 弓 (bow) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 单 originally indicated the sound.
