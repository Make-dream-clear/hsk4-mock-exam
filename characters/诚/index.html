诚 (chéng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

诚
chéng honest, sincere, true; actually, really 8 strokes · Radical 讠 (words) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 诚 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

诚 (chéng) means honest, sincere, true; actually, really. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 讠 (words), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced chéng.

  • honest
  • sincere
  • true
  • actually
  • really

Decomposition

诚 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

诚 =
讠 left words
成 right to accomplish

Radical

The radical of 诚 is 讠 — meaning words. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 误计许谈讨论订谅评详译谊.

How the character is built

诚 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 讠 (speech) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 成 originally indicated the sound.
