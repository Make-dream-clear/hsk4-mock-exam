袜 (wà) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

袜
wà socks, stockings 10 strokes · Radical 衤 (cloth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 袜 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

袜 (wà) means socks, stockings. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 衤 (cloth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced wà.

  • socks
  • stockings

Decomposition

袜 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

袜 =
衤 left cloth
末 right end

Radical

The radical of 袜 is 衤 — meaning cloth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

袜 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 衤 (clothes) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 末 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 袜

袜子 wàzi socks; stockings
这双袜子是纯棉的。
Zhè shuāng wàzi shì chúnmián de.
These socks are pure cotton.
