How to write 确 (què) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

确
què certain, sure; definite, exact; real, true 12 strokes · Radical 石 (stone) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

确 (què) means certain, sure; definite, exact; real, true. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 石 (stone), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #35 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced què.

  • certain
  • sure
  • definite
  • exact
  • real
  • true

Decomposition

确 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

确 =
石 left stone
角 right angle

Radical

The radical of 确 is 石 — meaning stone. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 研础破硕.

How the character is built

确 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 石 (stone) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 角 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 确

确实 quèshí indeed; really; truly
他说的话确实有道理。
Tā shuō de huà quèshí yǒu dàoli.
What he said indeed makes sense.
