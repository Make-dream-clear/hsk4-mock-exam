续 (xù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

续
xù continuous, serial 11 strokes · Radical 纟 (silk) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 续 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

续 (xù) means continuous, serial. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 纟 (silk), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xù.

  • continuous
  • serial

Decomposition

续 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

续 =
纟 left silk
卖 right to sell

Radical

The radical of 续 is 纟 — meaning silk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 线约组纪继绝细.

How the character is built

续 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 纟 (thread) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 卖 originally indicated the sound.
