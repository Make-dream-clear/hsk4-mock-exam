How to write 扔 (rēng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

扔
rēng to throw, to hurl, to cast away 5 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

扔 (rēng) means to throw, to hurl, to cast away. It is written in 5 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #110 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced rēng.

  • to throw
  • to hurl
  • to cast away

Decomposition

扔 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

扔 =
扌 left hand
乃 right then

Radical

The radical of 扔 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技掉拉指挂推排按播擦操.

How the character is built

扔 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 乃 originally indicated the sound.
