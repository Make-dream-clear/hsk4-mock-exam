仍 (réng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

仍
réng yet, still; keeping, continuing; again 4 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 仍 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

仍 (réng) means yet, still; keeping, continuing; again. It is written in 4 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced réng.

  • yet
  • still
  • keeping
  • continuing
  • again

Decomposition

仍 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

仍 =
亻 left man
乃 right then

Radical

The radical of 仍 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 保任使倒低例俩份停傲倍付.

How the character is built

仍 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 乃 (then) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 亻 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 仍

仍 réng still; yet; as before
虽然下雨了，比赛仍在进行。
Suīrán xià yǔ le, bǐsài réng zài jìnxíng.
Although it's raining, the game is still going on.
仍然 réngrán still; still remains; continue to
十年后他仍然住在这里。
Shí nián hòu tā réngrán zhù zài zhèlǐ.
He still lives here ten years later.
