降 (jiàng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

降
jiàng to descend, to fall; to drop, to lower 8 strokes · Radical 阝 (place) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 降 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

降 (jiàng) means to descend, to fall; to drop, to lower. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 阝 (place), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jiàng.

  • to descend
  • to fall
  • to drop
  • to lower

Decomposition

降 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

降 =
阝 left place
夅 right to descend

Radical

The radical of 降 is 阝 — meaning place. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 部际随队郊陪险.

How the character is built

降 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 阝 (hill) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 夅 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 降

降 jiàng to fall; to drop; to land
明天气温会降五度。
Míngtiān qìwēn huì jiàng wǔ dù.
The temperature will drop five degrees tomorrow.
降低 jiàngdī to reduce; to lower
超市降低了蔬菜的价格。
Chāoshì jiàngdī le shūcài de jiàgé.
The supermarket lowered vegetable prices.
降价 jiàngjià to reduce price; price drop
这款手机降价了。
Zhè kuǎn shǒujī jiàngjià le.
This phone has been discounted.
降落 jiàngluò to land; to descend
飞机安全降落在机场。
Fēijī ānquán jiàngluò zài jīchǎng.
The plane landed safely at the airport.
降温 jiàngwēn to cool down; temperature drop
明天要大幅降温。
Míngtiān yào dàfú jiàngwēn.
There will be a big temperature drop tomorrow.
下降 xiàjiàng to decline; to fall; to decrease
最近房价有所下降。
Zuìjìn fángjià yǒusuǒ xiàjiàng.
Housing prices have declined recently.
