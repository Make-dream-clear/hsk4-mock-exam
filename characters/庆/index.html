庆 (qìng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

庆
qìng to congratulate, to celebrate 6 strokes · Radical 广 (broad) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 庆 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

庆 (qìng) means to congratulate, to celebrate. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 广 (broad), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced qìng.

  • to congratulate
  • to celebrate

Decomposition

庆 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

庆 = 广 outer broad
大 inner big

Radical

The radical of 庆 is 广 — meaning broad. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 度庭座广底序.

How the character is built

庆 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A great 大 celebration in a house 广.
