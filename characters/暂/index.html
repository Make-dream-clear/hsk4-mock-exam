暂 (zàn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

暂
zàn temporary 12 strokes · Radical 日 (sun) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 暂 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

暂 (zàn) means temporary. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 日 (sun), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zàn.

temporary

Decomposition

暂 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

暂 =
斩 top to chop
日 bottom sun

Radical

The radical of 暂 is 日 — meaning sun. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 普晨景暖暑.

How the character is built

暂 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 日 (sun) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 斩 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 暂

暂时 zànshí temporary; for the time being
他暂时住在朋友家。
Tā zànshí zhù zài péngyou jiā.
He is temporarily staying at a friend's place.
暂停 zàntíng to pause; temporar y suspension
比赛暂停了五分钟。
Bǐsài zàntíng le wǔ fēnzhōng.
The game was paused for five minutes.
