How to write 保 (bǎo) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

保
bǎo to safeguard, to protect, to defend, to care for 9 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

保 (bǎo) means to safeguard, to protect, to defend, to care for. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #28 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced bǎo.

  • to safeguard
  • to protect
  • to defend
  • to care for

Decomposition

保 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

保 =
亻 left man
呆 right dull

Radical

The radical of 保 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 任使倒低例俩份停傲倍付傅.

How the character is built

保 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 亻 (person) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 呆 originally indicated the sound.
