傲 (ào) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

傲
ào proud, haughty, overbearing 12 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 傲 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

傲 (ào) means proud, haughty, overbearing. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced ào.

  • proud
  • haughty
  • overbearing

Decomposition

傲 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

傲 =
亻 left man
敖 right to ramble

Radical

The radical of 傲 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 保任使倒低例俩份停倍付傅.

How the character is built

傲 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 亻 (person) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 敖 originally indicated the sound.
