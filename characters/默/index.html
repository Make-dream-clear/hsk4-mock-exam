默 (mò) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

默
mò silent; quiet, still; dark 16 strokes · Radical 黑 (black) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 默 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

默 (mò) means silent; quiet, still; dark. It is written in 16 strokes, with the radical 黑 (black), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced mò.

  • silent
  • quiet
  • still
  • dark

Decomposition

默 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

默 =
黑 left black
犬 right dog

Radical

The radical of 默 is 黑 — meaning black. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

默 is a pictograph — a stylized image of the thing it names. It originally depicted A dog 犬 watching in the dark 黑.

HSK 4 Words Containing 默

幽默 yōumò humorous; witty

Tā shì yí gè fēicháng yōumò de rén.
He is a very humorous person.
