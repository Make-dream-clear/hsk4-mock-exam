缺 (quē) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

缺
quē to lack, to be short; vacancy, gap, deficit 10 strokes · Radical 缶 (earthen crock or jar) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 缺 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

缺 (quē) means to lack, to be short; vacancy, gap, deficit. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 缶 (earthen crock or jar), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced quē.

  • to lack
  • to be short
  • vacancy
  • gap
  • deficit

Decomposition

缺 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

缺 =
缶 left earthen crock or jar
夬 right decisive

Radical

The radical of 缺 is 缶 — meaning earthen crock or jar. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

缺 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 缶 (jar) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 夬 originally indicated the sound.
