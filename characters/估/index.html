估 (gū) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

估
gū merchant; to estimate, to guess, to presume 7 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 估 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

估 (gū) means merchant; to estimate, to guess, to presume. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced gū.

  • merchant
  • to estimate
  • to guess
  • to presume

Decomposition

估 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

估 =
亻 left man
古 right old

Radical

The radical of 估 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 保任使倒低例俩份停傲倍付.

How the character is built

估 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 亻 (person) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 古 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 估

估计 gūjì to estimate; to reckon
我估计他今天不会来了。
Wǒ gūjì tā jīntiān bú huì lái le.
I estimate he won't come today.
