How to write 讨 (tǎo) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

讨
tǎo to haggle, to discuss; to demand, to ask for 5 strokes · Radical 讠 (words) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

讨 (tǎo) means to haggle, to discuss; to demand, to ask for. It is written in 5 strokes, with the radical 讠 (words), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #87 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tǎo.

  • to haggle
  • to discuss
  • to demand
  • to ask for

Decomposition

讨 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

讨 =
讠 left words
寸 right inch

Radical

The radical of 讨 is 讠 — meaning words. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 误计许谈论诚订谅评详译谊.

How the character is built

讨 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. To ask 讠 for something 寸.

HSK 4 Words Containing 讨

讨论 tǎolùn to discuss; discussion
大家一起讨论这个方案。
Dàjiā yìqǐ tǎolùn zhège fāng'àn.
Everyone discusses this plan together.
讨厌 tǎoyàn to dislike; annoying; hateful
我最讨厌下雨天。
Wǒ zuì tǎoyàn xià yǔ tiān.
I hate rainy days the most.
