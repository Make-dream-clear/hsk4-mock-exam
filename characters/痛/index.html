How to write 痛 (tòng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

痛
tòng ache, pain; bitterness, sorrow; deeply, thoroughly 12 strokes · Radical 疒 (sickness) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

痛 (tòng) means ache, pain; bitterness, sorrow; deeply, thoroughly. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 疒 (sickness), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #92 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tòng.

  • ache
  • pain
  • bitterness
  • sorrow
  • deeply
  • thoroughly

Decomposition

痛 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

痛 =
疒 outer sickness
甬 inner path

Radical

The radical of 痛 is 疒 — meaning sickness. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

痛 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 疒 (sickness) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 甬 originally indicated the sound.
