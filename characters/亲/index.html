亲 (qīn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

亲
qīn relatives, parents; intimate; the hazelnut tree 9 strokes · Radical 立 (to stand) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 亲 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

亲 (qīn) means relatives, parents; intimate; the hazelnut tree. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 立 (to stand), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced qīn.

  • relatives
  • parents
  • intimate
  • the hazelnut tree

Decomposition

亲 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

亲 =
立 top to stand
一 bottom one
小 part small

Radical

The radical of 亲 is 立 — meaning to stand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 章竞竟童.

How the character is built

亲 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A tree 木 bearing fruit 立.

HSK 4 Words Containing 亲

我的父亲是一名工程师。
Wǒ de fùqīn shì yì míng gōngchéngshī.
My father is an engineer.
母亲 mǔqīn mother
母亲的爱是最伟大的。
Mǔqīn de ài shì zuì wěidà de.
A mother's love is the greatest.
亲戚 qīnqi relative; relation
过年的时候要去拜访亲戚。
Guònián de shíhou yào qù bàifǎng qīnqi.
You should visit relatives during New Year.
