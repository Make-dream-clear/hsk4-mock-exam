How to write 虎 (hǔ) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

虎
hǔ tiger; brave, fierce; surname 8 strokes · Radical 虎 (tiger) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

虎 (hǔ) means tiger; brave, fierce; surname. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 虎 (tiger), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #100 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced hǔ.

  • tiger
  • brave
  • fierce
  • surname

Decomposition

虎 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

虎 =
虍 outer tiger
几 inner small table

Radical

The radical of 虎 is 虎 — meaning tiger. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

虎 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A tiger 虍 standing on a rock 几; 虍 also provides the pronunciation.
