How to write 资 (zī) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

资
zī wealth, property, capital 10 strokes · Radical 贝 (sea shell) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

资 (zī) means wealth, property, capital. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 贝 (sea shell), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #93 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zī.

  • wealth
  • property
  • capital

Decomposition

资 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

资 =
次 top order
贝 bottom sea shell

Radical

The radical of 资 is 贝 — meaning sea shell. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 败费负购贺货赢责账赚.

How the character is built

资 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 贝 (money) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 次 originally indicated the sound.
