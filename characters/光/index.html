光 (guāng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

光
guāng light; bright, brilliant; only, merely 6 strokes · Radical 儿 (son) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 光 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

光 (guāng) means light; bright, brilliant; only, merely. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 儿 (son), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced guāng.

  • light
  • bright
  • brilliant
  • only
  • merely

Decomposition

光 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

光 =
⺌ top
兀 bottom weak

Radical

The radical of 光 is 儿 — meaning son. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 兄克免允.

How the character is built

光 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A person 儿 carrying a torch 火.

HSK 4 Words Containing 光

不光 bùguāng not only; not just
不光是我，大家都这么想。
Bùguāng shì wǒ, dàjiā dōu zhème xiǎng.
Not just me, everyone thinks so.
光 guāng light; ray; only; merely
光说不做没有用。
Guāng shuō bú zuò méiyǒu yòng.
Just talking without doing is useless.
阳光 yángguāng sunshine; sunlight
今天阳光很好，适合出去走走。
Jīntiān yángguāng hěn hǎo, shìhé chūqu zǒuzou.
The sunshine is nice today, good for a walk.
