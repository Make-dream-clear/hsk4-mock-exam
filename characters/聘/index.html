聘 (pìn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

聘
pìn to employ, to engage; betrothed 13 strokes · Radical 耳 (ear) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 聘 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

聘 (pìn) means to employ, to engage; betrothed. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical 耳 (ear), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced pìn.

  • to employ
  • to engage
  • betrothed

Decomposition

聘 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

聘 =
耳 left ear
甹 right chivalrous knight

Radical

The radical of 聘 is 耳 — meaning ear. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 联聚职.

How the character is built

聘 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 耳 (ear) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 甹 originally indicated the sound.

应聘 yìngpìn to apply for a job

招聘 zhāopìn to recruit; to hire
这家公司正在招聘工程师。
Zhè jiā gōngsī zhèngzài zhāopìn gōngchéngshī.
This company is recruiting engineers.
