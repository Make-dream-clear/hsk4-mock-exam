勇 (yǒng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

勇
yǒng brave, courageous, fierce 9 strokes · Radical 力 (strength) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 勇 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

勇 (yǒng) means brave, courageous, fierce. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 力 (strength), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yǒng.

  • brave
  • courageous
  • fierce

Decomposition

勇 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

勇 =
甬 top path
力 bottom strength

Radical

The radical of 勇 is 力 — meaning strength. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 功励.

How the character is built

勇 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 力 (strength) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 甬 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 勇

他是一个勇敢的人。
Tā shì yí gè yǒnggǎn de rén.
He is a brave person.
