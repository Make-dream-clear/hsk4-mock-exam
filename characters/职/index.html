职 (zhí) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

职
zhí duty, profession; office, post 11 strokes · Radical 耳 (ear) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 职 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

职 (zhí) means duty, profession; office, post. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 耳 (ear), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhí.

  • duty
  • profession
  • office
  • post

Decomposition

职 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

职 =
耳 left ear
只 right only

Radical

The radical of 职 is 耳 — meaning ear. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 联聚聘.

How the character is built

职 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 耳 (ear) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 只 originally indicated the sound.
