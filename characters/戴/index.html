戴 (dài) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

戴
dài to support; to respect; to put on, to wear; surname 17 strokes · Radical 戈 (spear) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 戴 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

戴 (dài) means to support; to respect; to put on, to wear; surname. It is written in 17 strokes, with the radical 戈 (spear), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced dài.

  • to support
  • to respect
  • to put on
  • to wear
  • surname

Radical

The radical of 戴 is 戈 — meaning spear. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 戚.

HSK 4 Words Containing 戴
