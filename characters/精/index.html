精 (jīng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

精
jīng essence, germ, spirit 14 strokes · Radical 米 (rice) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 精 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

精 (jīng) means essence, germ, spirit. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 米 (rice), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jīng.

  • essence
  • germ
  • spirit

Decomposition

精 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

精 =
米 left rice
青 right nature's color

Radical

The radical of 精 is 米 — meaning rice. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 粗.

How the character is built

精 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 米 (grain) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 青 originally indicated the sound.
