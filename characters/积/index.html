积 (jī) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

积
jī to store up, to amass, to accumulate 10 strokes · Radical 禾 (cereal) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 积 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

积 (jī) means to store up, to amass, to accumulate. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 禾 (cereal), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jī.

  • to store up
  • to amass
  • to accumulate

Decomposition

积 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

积 =
禾 left cereal
只 right only

Radical

The radical of 积 is 禾 — meaning cereal. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 程科秒稍秀.

How the character is built

积 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 禾 (grain) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 只 originally indicated the sound.
