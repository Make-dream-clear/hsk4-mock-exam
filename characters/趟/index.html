趟 (tàng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

趟
tàng time, occasion; to make a journey 15 strokes · Radical 走 (to walk) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 趟 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

趟 (tàng) means time, occasion; to make a journey. It is written in 15 strokes, with the radical 走 (to walk), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tàng.

  • time
  • occasion
  • to make a journey

Decomposition

趟 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Lower-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

趟 =
走 outer to walk
尚 inner still

Radical

The radical of 趟 is 走 — meaning to walk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 赶.

How the character is built

趟 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 走 (walk) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 尚 originally indicated the sound.
