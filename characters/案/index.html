案 (àn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

案
àn file, legal case; bench, table 10 strokes · Radical 木 (tree) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 案 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

案 (àn) means file, legal case; bench, table. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 木 (tree), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced àn.

  • file
  • legal case
  • bench
  • table

Decomposition

案 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

案 =
安 top peaceful
木 bottom tree

Radical

The radical of 案 is 木 — meaning tree. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 标村术棒材格棵林梦桥染森.

How the character is built

案 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 木 (wood) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 安 originally indicated the sound.
