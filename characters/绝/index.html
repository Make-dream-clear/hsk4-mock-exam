绝 (jué) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

绝
jué to cut, to sever; to break off, to terminate 9 strokes · Radical 纟 (silk) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 绝 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

绝 (jué) means to cut, to sever; to break off, to terminate. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 纟 (silk), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jué.

  • to cut
  • to sever
  • to break off
  • to terminate

Decomposition

绝 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

绝 =
纟 left silk
色 right color

Radical

The radical of 绝 is 纟 — meaning silk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 线约组纪继细续.

How the character is built

绝 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 纟 (thread) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 色 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 绝

拒绝 jùjué to refuse; to decline
他拒绝了对方的邀请。
Tā jùjué le duìfāng de yāoqǐng.
He declined the other party's invitation.
