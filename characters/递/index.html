递 (dì) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

递
dì to deliver, to hand over; substitute 10 strokes · Radical 辶 (to walk) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 递 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

递 (dì) means to deliver, to hand over; substitute. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 辶 (to walk), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced dì.

  • to deliver
  • to hand over
  • substitute

Decomposition

递 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Lower-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

递 =
辶 outer to walk
弟 inner young brother

Radical

The radical of 递 is 辶 — meaning to walk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 达连通逛迷速邀.

How the character is built

递 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 辶 (walk) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 弟 originally indicated the sound.
