How to write 兄 (xiōng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

兄
xiōng elder brother 5 strokes · Radical 儿 (son) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

兄 (xiōng) means elder brother. It is written in 5 strokes, with the radical 儿 (son), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #105 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xiōng.

elder brother

Decomposition

兄 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

兄 =
口 top mouth
儿 bottom son

Radical

The radical of 兄 is 儿 — meaning son. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 克光免允.

How the character is built

兄 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Older brother 儿 speaking 口 to younger ones.
