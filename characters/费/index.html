费 (fèi) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

费
fèi expenses, fees; to cost, to spend; wasteful 9 strokes · Radical 贝 (sea shell) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 费 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

费 (fèi) means expenses, fees; to cost, to spend; wasteful. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 贝 (sea shell), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced fèi.

  • expenses
  • fees
  • to cost
  • to spend
  • wasteful

Decomposition

费 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

费 =
弗 top not
贝 bottom sea shell

Radical

The radical of 费 is 贝 — meaning sea shell. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 资败负购贺货赢责账赚.

How the character is built

费 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 贝 (money) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 弗 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 费

费 fèi fee; charge; expense
这次旅行的费用不高。
Zhè cì lǚxíng de fèiyong bù gāo.
The cost of this trip is not high.
费用 fèiyong cost; expense; fee
留学的费用越来越高。
Liúxué de fèiyong yuèláiyuè gāo.
The cost of studying abroad is getting higher.
浪费 làngfèi to waste
不要浪费粮食。
Búyào làngfèi liángshi.
Don't waste food.
免费 miǎnfèi free of charge; for free
这个公园是免费的。
Zhège gōngyuán shì miǎnfèi de.
This park is free of charge.
收费 shōufèi to charge a fee
这个停车场怎么收费？
Zhège tíngchēchǎng zěnme shōufèi?
How does this parking lot charge?
学费 xuéfèi tuition fee; school fee
大学的学费一年多少钱？
Dàxué de xuéfèi yì nián duōshao qián?
How much is the annual tuition for university?
