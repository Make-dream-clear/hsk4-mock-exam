How to write 章 (zhāng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

章
zhāng chapter, section, writing; seal 11 strokes · Radical 立 (to stand) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

章 (zhāng) means chapter, section, writing; seal. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 立 (to stand), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #133 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhāng.

  • chapter
  • section
  • writing
  • seal

章 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

Radical

The radical of 章 is 立 — meaning to stand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 竞竟亲童.

How the character is built

章 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. The ten 十 movements of a piece of music 音.

HSK 4 Words Containing 章

Tā xiě le yì piān hěn hǎo de wénzhāng.
He wrote a very good article.
