整 (zhěng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

整
zhěng neat, orderly, whole; to repair, to mend 16 strokes · Radical 攵 (to rap) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 整 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

整 (zhěng) means neat, orderly, whole; to repair, to mend. It is written in 16 strokes, with the radical 攵 (to rap), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhěng.

  • neat
  • orderly
  • whole
  • to repair
  • to mend

Decomposition

整 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

整 =
敕 top an imperial order or decree
正 bottom straight

Radical

The radical of 整 is 攵 — meaning to rap. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 改敢散效.

How the character is built

整 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 敕 (order) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 正 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 整

整 zhěng whole; entire; complete
他等了整整三个小时。
Tā děng le zhěngzhěng sān gè xiǎoshí.
He waited for a full three hours.
整个 zhěnggè whole; entire; overall
整个城市都在下雪。
Zhěnggè chéngshì dōu zài xià xuě.
The entire city is snowing.
整理 zhěnglǐ to organize; to sort out; to tidy
他正在整理房间。
Tā zhèngzài zhěnglǐ fángjiān.
He is tidying up the room.
