呼 (hū) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

呼
hū to breathe, to exhale, to sigh; to call, to shout 8 strokes · Radical 口 (mouth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 呼 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

呼 (hū) means to breathe, to exhale, to sigh; to call, to shout. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 口 (mouth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced hū.

  • to breathe
  • to exhale
  • to sigh
  • to call
  • to shout

Decomposition

呼 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

呼 =
口 left mouth
乎 right interrogative or exclamatory final particle

Radical

The radical of 呼 is 口 — meaning mouth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 品吹台否另各味喊咳命嗯售.

How the character is built

呼 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 口 (mouth) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 乎 originally indicated the sound.
