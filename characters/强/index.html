How to write 强 (qiáng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

强
qiáng strong, powerful, energetic 12 strokes · Radical 弓 (bow) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

强 (qiáng) means strong, powerful, energetic. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 弓 (bow), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #60 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced qiáng.

  • strong
  • powerful
  • energetic

Decomposition

强 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

强 =
弓 left bow
虽 right although

Radical

The radical of 强 is 弓 — meaning bow. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 弹引.

How the character is built

强 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 弓 (bow) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 虫 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 强

加强 jiāqiáng to strengthen; to enhance
学校加强了安全管理。
Xuéxiào jiāqiáng le ānquán guǎnlǐ.
The school strengthened security management.
强 qiáng strong; powerful
他的记忆力很强。
Tā de jìyìlì hěn qiáng.
He has a very strong memory.
