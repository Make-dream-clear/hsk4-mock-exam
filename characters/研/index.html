How to write 研 (yán) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

研
yán to grind, to rub; to study, to research 9 strokes · Radical 石 (stone) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

研 (yán) means to grind, to rub; to study, to research. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 石 (stone), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #66 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yán.

  • to grind
  • to rub
  • to study
  • to research

Decomposition

研 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

研 =
石 left stone
开 right to open

Radical

The radical of 研 is 石 — meaning stone. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 确础破硕.

How the character is built

研 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 石 (stone) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 开 originally indicated the sound.
