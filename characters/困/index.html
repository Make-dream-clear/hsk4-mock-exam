How to write 困 (kùn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

困
kùn to surround; to besiege; surrounded, in distress; poor; tired, sleepy 7 strokes · Radical 囗 (enclosure) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

困 (kùn) means to surround; to besiege; surrounded, in distress; poor; tired, sleepy. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 囗 (enclosure), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #73 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced kùn.

  • to surround
  • to besiege
  • surrounded
  • in distress
  • poor
  • tired

Decomposition

困 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Outer surrounds Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

困 =
囗 outer enclosure
木 inner tree

Radical

The radical of 困 is 囗 — meaning enclosure. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 围.

How the character is built

困 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A fence 囗 built around a tree 木.

HSK 4 Words Containing 困

困 kùn sleepy; drowsy
我很困，想睡觉了。
Wǒ hěn kùn, xiǎng shuìjiào le.
I'm sleepy, I want to go to bed.

学中文虽然困难，但很有趣。
Xué Zhōngwén suīrán kùnnan, dàn hěn yǒuqù.
Learning Chinese is difficult but interesting.
