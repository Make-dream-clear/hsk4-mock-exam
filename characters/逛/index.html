逛 (guàng) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

逛
guàng to ramble, to stroll, to wander 10 strokes · Radical 辶 (to walk) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 逛 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

逛 (guàng) means to ramble, to stroll, to wander. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 辶 (to walk), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced guàng.

  • to ramble
  • to stroll
  • to wander

Decomposition

逛 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Lower-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

逛 =
辶 outer to walk
狂 inner insane

Radical

The radical of 逛 is 辶 — meaning to walk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 达连通递迷速邀.

How the character is built

逛 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 辶 (walk) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 狂 originally indicated the sound.
