How to write 座 (zuò) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

座
zuò seat; stand, base 10 strokes · Radical 广 (broad) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

座 (zuò) means seat; stand, base. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 广 (broad), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #95 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zuò.

  • seat
  • stand
  • base

Decomposition

座 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

座 = 广 outer broad
坐 inner seat

Radical

The radical of 座 is 广 — meaning broad. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 度庭广底庆序.

How the character is built

座 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A wide 广 seat 坐; 坐 also provides the pronunciation.

HSK 4 Words Containing 座

座 zuò seat; position
前面有一座大山。
Qiánmiàn yǒu yí zuò dà shān.
There is a big mountain ahead.
