负 (fù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

负
fù load, burden; to carry, to bear 6 strokes · Radical 贝 (sea shell) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 负 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

负 (fù) means load, burden; to carry, to bear. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 贝 (sea shell), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced fù.

  • load
  • burden
  • to carry
  • to bear

Decomposition

负 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

负 =
⺈ top
贝 bottom sea shell

Radical

The radical of 负 is 贝 — meaning sea shell. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 资败费购贺货赢责账赚.

How the character is built

负 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A person 人 carrying a lot of money 贝.
