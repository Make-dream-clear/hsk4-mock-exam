How to write 烦 (fán) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

烦
fán to bother, to trouble, to vex 10 strokes · Radical 火 (fire) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

烦 (fán) means to bother, to trouble, to vex. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 火 (fire), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #46 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced fán.

  • to bother
  • to trouble
  • to vex

Decomposition

烦 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

烦 =
火 left fire
页 right page

Radical

The radical of 烦 is 火 — meaning fire. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 烤烟.

How the character is built

烦 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 火 (fire) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 页 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 烦

烦 fán annoyed; fed up
这道题太烦了。
Zhè dào tí tài fán le.
This problem is so annoying.
烦恼 fánnǎo worried; troubled; upset
每个人都有自己的烦恼。
Měi gè rén dōu yǒu zìjǐ de fánnǎo.
Everyone has their own worries.
麻烦 máfan troublesome; to trouble; trouble (n)
麻烦你帮我一下。
Máfan nǐ bāng wǒ yīxià.
Sorry to trouble you, please help me.
