How to write 究 (jiū) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

究
jiū to dig into, to investigate; actually, after all 7 strokes · Radical 穴 (cave) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

究 (jiū) means to dig into, to investigate; actually, after all. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 穴 (cave), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #39 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jiū.

  • to dig into
  • to investigate
  • actually
  • after all

Decomposition

究 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

究 =
穴 top cave
九 bottom nine

Radical

The radical of 究 is 穴 — meaning cave. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 窗.

How the character is built

究 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 穴 (cave) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 九 originally indicated the sound.
