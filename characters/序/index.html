序 (xù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

序
xù order, sequence, series; preface 7 strokes · Radical 广 (broad) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 序 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

序 (xù) means order, sequence, series; preface. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 广 (broad), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xù.

  • order
  • sequence
  • series
  • preface

Decomposition

序 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

序 = 广 outer broad
予 inner to give

Radical

The radical of 序 is 广 — meaning broad. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 度庭座广底庆.

How the character is built

序 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 广 (wide) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 予 originally indicated the sound.
