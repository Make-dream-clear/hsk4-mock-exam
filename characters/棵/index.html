棵 (kē) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

棵
kē measure word for trees 12 strokes · Radical 木 (tree) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 棵 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

棵 (kē) means measure word for trees. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 木 (tree), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced kē.

Decomposition

棵 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

棵 =
木 left tree
果 right fruit

Radical

The radical of 棵 is 木 — meaning tree. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 标村术案棒材格林梦桥染森.

How the character is built

棵 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 木 (tree) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 果 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 棵

棵 kē (measure word: trees, plants)
院子里种了一棵大树。
Yuànzi lǐ zhòng le yì kē dà shù.
A big tree was planted in the yard.
