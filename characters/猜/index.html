猜 (cāi) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

猜
cāi to guess, to conjecture, to suppose; to feel 11 strokes · Radical 犭 (dog) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 猜 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

猜 (cāi) means to guess, to conjecture, to suppose; to feel. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 犭 (dog), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced cāi.

  • to guess
  • to conjecture
  • to suppose
  • to feel

Decomposition

猜 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

猜 =
犭 left dog
青 right nature's color

Radical

The radical of 猜 is 犭 — meaning dog. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

猜 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 犭 (dog) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 青 originally indicated the sound.
