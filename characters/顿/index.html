顿 (dùn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

顿
dùn to pause; to bow; to arrange 10 strokes · Radical 页 (page) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 顿 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

顿 (dùn) means to pause; to bow; to arrange. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 页 (page), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced dùn.

  • to pause
  • to bow
  • to arrange

Decomposition

顿 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

顿 =
屯 left village
页 right page

Radical

The radical of 顿 is 页 — meaning page. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 顺频项预.

How the character is built

顿 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 页 (head) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 屯 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 顿

顿 dùn (measure word: meals); to pause
我们一天吃三顿饭。
Wǒmen yì tiān chī sān dùn fàn.
We eat three meals a day.
