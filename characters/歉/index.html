歉 (qiàn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

歉
qiàn deficient, lacking; to apologize, to regret, to be sorry 14 strokes · Radical 欠 (to lack) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 歉 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

歉 (qiàn) means deficient, lacking; to apologize, to regret, to be sorry. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 欠 (to lack), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced qiàn.

  • deficient
  • lacking
  • to apologize
  • to regret
  • to be sorry

Decomposition

歉 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

歉 =
兼 left both
欠 right to lack

Radical

The radical of 歉 is 欠 — meaning to lack. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

歉 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 欠 (lack) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 兼 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 歉

抱歉 bàoqiàn sorry; apologetic
非常抱歉，让你久等了。
Fēicháng bàoqiàn, ràng nǐ jiǔ děng le.
Very sorry to have kept you waiting.
道歉 dàoqiàn to apologize
你应该向他道歉。
Nǐ yīnggāi xiàng tā dàoqiàn.
You should apologize to him.
