How to write 改 (gǎi) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

改
gǎi to alter, to change, to improve, to remodel 7 strokes · Radical 攵 (to rap) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

改 (gǎi) means to alter, to change, to improve, to remodel. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 攵 (to rap), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #88 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced gǎi.

  • to alter
  • to change
  • to improve
  • to remodel

Decomposition

改 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

改 =
己 left self
攵 right to rap

Radical

The radical of 改 is 攵 — meaning to rap. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 敢散效整.

How the character is built

改 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A hand with a stick 攵 disciplining a kneeling child 己.
