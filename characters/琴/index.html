琴 (qín) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

琴
qín Chinese lute or guitar, string instrument 12 strokes · Radical 王 (king) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 琴 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

琴 (qín) means Chinese lute or guitar, string instrument. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 王 (king), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced qín.

  • Chinese lute or guitar
  • string instrument

Decomposition

琴 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

琴 =
玨 top guitar strings
今 bottom modern

Radical

The radical of 琴 is 王 — meaning king. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

琴 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Two guitar strings 玨 suggest the meaning; 今 provides the pronunciation.
