How to write 掉 (diào) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

掉
diào to drop, to fall, to remove 11 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

掉 (diào) means to drop, to fall, to remove. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #130 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced diào.

  • to drop
  • to fall
  • to remove

Decomposition

掉 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

掉 =
扌 left hand
卓 right profound

Radical

The radical of 掉 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔拉指挂推排按播擦操.

How the character is built

掉 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 卓 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 掉

掉 diào to fall; to drop; to lose
他的钱包掉在地上了。
Tā de qiánbāo diào zài dì shàng le.
His wallet fell on the ground.
