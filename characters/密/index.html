密 (mì) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

密
mì secret, confidential; intimate, close; dense, thick 11 strokes · Radical 宀 (roof) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 密 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

密 (mì) means secret, confidential; intimate, close; dense, thick. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 宀 (roof), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced mì.

  • secret
  • confidential
  • intimate
  • close
  • dense
  • thick

Decomposition

密 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

密 =
宓 top quiet
山 bottom mountain

Radical

The radical of 密 is 宀 — meaning roof. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 寄察富寒.

How the character is built

密 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A silent 宓 mountain 山; 宓 also provides the pronunciation.
