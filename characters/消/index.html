消 (xiāo) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

消
xiāo news, rumors; to die out, to melt away, to vanish 10 strokes · Radical 氵 (water) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 消 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

消 (xiāo) means news, rumors; to die out, to melt away, to vanish. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 氵 (water), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced xiāo.

  • news
  • rumors
  • to die out
  • to melt away
  • to vanish

Decomposition

消 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

消 =
氵 left water
肖 right to resemble

Radical

The radical of 消 is 氵 — meaning water. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 济江深油汤流温汗浪漫泼污.

How the character is built

消 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 氵 (water) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 肖 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 消

取消 qǔxiāo to cancel
因为天气原因，航班被取消了。
Yīnwèi tiānqì yuányīn, hángbān bèi qǔxiāo le.
The flight was cancelled due to weather.
消息 xiāoxi news; message; information
你听到那个好消息了吗？
Nǐ tīngdào nàge hǎo xiāoxi le ma?
Did you hear the good news?
