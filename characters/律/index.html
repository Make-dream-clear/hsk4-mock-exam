律 (lǜ) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

律
lǜ statute, principle, regulation 9 strokes · Radical 彳 (to step with the left foot) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 律 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

律 (lǜ) means statute, principle, regulation. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 彳 (to step with the left foot), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced lǜ.

  • statute
  • principle
  • regulation

Decomposition

律 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

律 =
彳 left to step with the left foot
聿 right writing brush

Radical

The radical of 律 is 彳 — meaning to step with the left foot. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 待微.

How the character is built

律 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. To walk 彳 as required by law 聿.

HSK 4 Words Containing 律

法律 fǎlǜ law; legislation
每个公民都要遵守法律。
Měi gè gōngmín dōu yào zūnshǒu fǎlǜ.
Every citizen must obey the law.
律师 lǜshī lawyer
他的哥哥是一位律师。
Tā de gēge shì yí wèi lǜshī.
His older brother is a lawyer.
