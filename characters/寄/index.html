How to write 寄 (jì) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

寄
jì to mail, to send, to transit; to deposit, to entrust; to rely on 11 strokes · Radical 宀 (roof) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

寄 (jì) means to mail, to send, to transit; to deposit, to entrust; to rely on. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 宀 (roof), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #109 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jì.

  • to mail
  • to send
  • to transit
  • to deposit
  • to entrust
  • to rely on

Decomposition

寄 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

寄 =
宀 top roof
奇 bottom strange

Radical

The radical of 寄 is 宀 — meaning roof. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 察富寒密.

How the character is built

寄 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 宀 (roof) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 奇 originally indicated the sound.
