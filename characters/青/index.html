How to write 青 (qīng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

青
qīng nature's color; blue, green, black; young 8 strokes · Radical 青 (nature's color) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

青 (qīng) means nature's color; blue, green, black; young. It is written in 8 strokes, with the radical 青 (nature's color), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #104 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced qīng.

  • nature's color
  • blue
  • green
  • black
  • young

The radical of 青 is 青 — meaning nature's color. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.
