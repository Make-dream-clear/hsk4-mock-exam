贺 (hè) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

贺
hè to congratulate; to send a present 9 strokes · Radical 贝 (sea shell) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 贺 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

贺 (hè) means to congratulate; to send a present. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 贝 (sea shell), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced hè.

  • to congratulate
  • to send a present

Decomposition

贺 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

贺 =
加 top to add to
贝 bottom sea shell

Radical

The radical of 贺 is 贝 — meaning sea shell. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 资败费负购货赢责账赚.

How the character is built

贺 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. To add 加 to someone else's fortune 贝.
