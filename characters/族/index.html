族 (zú) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

族
zú race, nationality, ethnicity; tribe, clan 11 strokes · Radical 方 (square) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 族 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

族 (zú) means race, nationality, ethnicity; tribe, clan. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 方 (square), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zú.

  • race
  • nationality
  • ethnicity
  • tribe
  • clan

Decomposition

族 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

族 =
方 left square
矢 right arrow

Radical

The radical of 族 is 方 — meaning square. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

族 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A group of people that swear by 矢 a single flag 方.

HSK 4 Words Containing 族

民族 mínzú ethnic group; nationality
中国有五十六个民族。
Zhōngguó yǒu wǔshíliù gè mínzú.
China has 56 ethnic groups.
