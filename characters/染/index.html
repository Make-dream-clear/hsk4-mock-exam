染 (rǎn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

染
rǎn dye; to catch; to infect; to be contagious 9 strokes · Radical 木 (tree) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 染 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

染 (rǎn) means dye; to catch; to infect; to be contagious. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 木 (tree), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced rǎn.

  • dye
  • to catch
  • to infect
  • to be contagious

Decomposition

染 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

染 =
氿 top spring
木 bottom tree

Radical

The radical of 染 is 木 — meaning tree. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 标村术案棒材格棵林梦桥森.

How the character is built

染 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. Water 氵 and wine 九 mixed in a wooden 木 bowl.
