How to write 达 (dá) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

达
dá to reach, to arrive at; intelligent 6 strokes · Radical 辶 (to walk) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

达 (dá) means to reach, to arrive at; intelligent. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 辶 (to walk), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #122 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced dá.

  • to reach
  • to arrive at
  • intelligent

Decomposition

达 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Lower-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

达 =
辶 outer to walk
大 inner big

Radical

The radical of 达 is 辶 — meaning to walk. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 连通递逛迷速邀.

How the character is built

达 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 辶 carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 大 originally indicated the sound.
