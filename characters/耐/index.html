耐 (nài) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

耐
nài to resist, to bear; patient, enduring 9 strokes · Radical 而 (and) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 耐 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

耐 (nài) means to resist, to bear; patient, enduring. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 而 (and), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced nài.

  • to resist
  • to bear
  • patient
  • enduring

Decomposition

耐 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

耐 =
而 left and
寸 right inch

Radical

The radical of 耐 is 而 — meaning and. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

HSK 4 Words Containing 耐

耐心 nàixīn patient; patience
教小朋友需要很大的耐心。

Teaching young children requires a lot of patience.
