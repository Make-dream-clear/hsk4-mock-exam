幅 (fú) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

幅
fú piece, roll, strip; breadth, width; measure word for textiles 12 strokes · Radical 巾 (cloth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 幅 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

幅 (fú) means piece, roll, strip; breadth, width; measure word for textiles. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 巾 (cloth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Decomposition

幅 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

幅 = 巾 left cloth
畐 right to fill

Radical

The radical of 幅 is 巾 — meaning cloth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 巾帽帅.

How the character is built

幅 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A roll 畐 of cloth 巾; 畐 also provides the pronunciation.

HSK 4 Words Containing 幅

幅 fú (measure word: paintings, cloth)
墙上挂着一幅画。
Qiáng shàng guà zhe yì fú huà.
There is a painting hanging on the wall.
