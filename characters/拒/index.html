拒 (jù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

拒
jù to defend, to ward off; to refuse 7 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 拒 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

拒 (jù) means to defend, to ward off; to refuse. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jù.

  • to defend
  • to ward off
  • to refuse

Decomposition

拒 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

拒 =
扌 left hand
巨 right large

Radical

The radical of 拒 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔掉拉指挂推排按播擦.

How the character is built

拒 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 巨 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 拒

拒绝 jùjué to refuse; to decline
他拒绝了对方的邀请。
Tā jùjué le duìfāng de yāoqǐng.
He declined the other party's invitation.
