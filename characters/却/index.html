却 (què) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

却
què still, but; decline; retreat 7 strokes · Radical 卩 (seal) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 却 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

却 (què) means still, but; decline; retreat. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 卩 (seal), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced què.

  • still
  • but
  • decline
  • retreat

Decomposition

却 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

却 =
去 left to go away
卩 right seal

Radical

The radical of 却 is 卩 — meaning seal. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 印即.

How the character is built

却 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 卩 (seal) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 去 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 却

却 què but; yet; however
他很聪明，成绩却不好。
Tā hěn cōngming, chéngjì què bù hǎo.
He is smart, yet his grades are not good.
