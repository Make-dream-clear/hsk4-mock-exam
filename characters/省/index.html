How to write 省 (shěng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

省
shěng province; frugal; to save, to leave out 9 strokes · Radical 目 (eye) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

省 (shěng) means province; frugal; to save, to leave out. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 目 (eye), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #119 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shěng.

  • province
  • frugal
  • to save
  • to leave out

Decomposition

省 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

省 =
少 top few
目 bottom eye

Radical

The radical of 省 is 目 — meaning eye. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

省 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 目 (division) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 少 originally indicated the sound.
