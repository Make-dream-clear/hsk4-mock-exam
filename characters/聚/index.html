聚 (jù) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

聚
jù to assemble, to collect, to meet 14 strokes · Radical 耳 (ear) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 聚 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

聚 (jù) means to assemble, to collect, to meet. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 耳 (ear), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced jù.

  • to assemble
  • to collect
  • to meet

Decomposition

聚 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

聚 = 取 top to take
乑 bottom to stand side by side

Radical

The radical of 聚 is 耳 — meaning ear. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 联聘职.

How the character is built

聚 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. People standing side-by-side 乑, hand-to-ear 取.

HSK 4 Words Containing 聚

聚 jù to gather; to congregate
老同学好不容易聚在一起。
Lǎo tóngxué hǎo bù róngyì jù zài yìqǐ.
Old classmates finally got together.
聚餐 jùcān to have a group meal; group dinner
部门下周五聚餐。
Bùmén xià zhōuwǔ jùcān.
The department will have a group dinner next Friday.
聚会 jùhuì to get together; gathering; party
我们周末开个聚会吧。
Wǒmen zhōumò kāi gè jùhuì ba.
Let's have a party this weekend.
