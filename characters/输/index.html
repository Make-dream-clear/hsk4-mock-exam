How to write 输 (shū) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

输
shū to carry, to haul, to transport 13 strokes · Radical 车 (cart) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

输 (shū) means to carry, to haul, to transport. It is written in 13 strokes, with the radical 车 (cart), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #128 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced shū.

  • to carry
  • to haul
  • to transport

Decomposition

输 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

输 =
车 left cart
俞 right to consent

Radical

The radical of 输 is 车 — meaning cart. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 转.

How the character is built

输 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 车 (cart) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 俞 originally indicated the sound.
