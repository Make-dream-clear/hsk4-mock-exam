何 (hé) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

何
hé what, why, where, which, how 7 strokes · Radical 亻 (man) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 何 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

何 (hé) means what, why, where, which, how. It is written in 7 strokes, with the radical 亻 (man), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced hé.

  • what
  • why
  • where
  • which
  • how

Decomposition

何 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

何 =
亻 left man
可 right may

Radical

The radical of 何 is 亻 — meaning man. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 保任使倒低例俩份停傲倍付.
