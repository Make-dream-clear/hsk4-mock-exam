How to write 敢 (gǎn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

敢
gǎn bold, brave; to dare, to venture 11 strokes · Radical 攵 (to rap) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

敢 (gǎn) means bold, brave; to dare, to venture. It is written in 11 strokes, with the radical 攵 (to rap), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #90 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced gǎn.

  • bold
  • brave
  • to dare
  • to venture

Decomposition

敢 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

敢 =
耳 left ear
攵 right to rap

Radical

The radical of 敢 is 攵 — meaning to rap. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 改散效整.

How the character is built

敢 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A hand with a stick 攵 striking a beast 耳.

HSK 4 Words Containing 敢

敢 gǎn to dare

Tā bù gǎn yí gè rén zǒu yèlù.
He doesn't dare walk alone at night.
勇敢 yǒnggǎn brave; courageous
他是一个勇敢的人。
Tā shì yí gè yǒnggǎn de rén.
He is a brave person.
