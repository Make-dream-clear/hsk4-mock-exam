赚 (zhuàn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

赚
zhuàn to earn, to profit, to make money 14 strokes · Radical 贝 (sea shell) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 赚 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

赚 (zhuàn) means to earn, to profit, to make money. It is written in 14 strokes, with the radical 贝 (sea shell), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhuàn.

  • to earn
  • to profit
  • to make money

Decomposition

赚 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

赚 =
贝 left sea shell
兼 right both

Radical

The radical of 赚 is 贝 — meaning sea shell. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 资败费负购贺货赢责账.

How the character is built

赚 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 贝 (money) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 兼 originally indicated the sound.
