How to write 庭 (tíng) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

庭
tíng court; courtyard; spacious hall or yard 9 strokes · Radical 广 (broad) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

庭 (tíng) means court; courtyard; spacious hall or yard. It is written in 9 strokes, with the radical 广 (broad), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #142 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced tíng.

  • court
  • courtyard
  • spacious hall or yard

Decomposition

庭 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

庭 = 广 outer broad
廷 inner courtyard

Radical

The radical of 庭 is 广 — meaning broad. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 度座广底庆序.

How the character is built

庭 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A wide 广 couryard 廷; 廷 also provides the pronunciation.
