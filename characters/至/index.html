How to write 至 (zhì) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

至
zhì reach, arrive; very, extremely 6 strokes · Radical 至 (reach) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

至 (zhì) means reach, arrive; very, extremely. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 至 (reach), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #85 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhì.

  • reach
  • arrive
  • very
  • extremely

Decomposition

至 breaks down into 3 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

至 =
一 top one
厶 bottom private
土 part soil

Radical

The radical of 至 is 至 — meaning reach. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

至 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A bird alighting on the ground 土.

HSK 4 Words Containing 至

甚至 shènzhì even; even to the point of
他很忙，甚至周末也在工作。
Tā hěn máng, shènzhì zhōumò yě zài gōngzuò.
He's very busy, he even works on weekends.
至少 zhìshǎo at least
这次旅行至少需要五天。
Zhè cì lǚxíng zhìshǎo xūyào wǔ tiān.
This trip requires at least five days.
