How to write 支 (zhī) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

支
zhī to support, to sustain; to withdraw, to pay; a branch (of a bank) 4 strokes · Radical 支 (to support) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

支 (zhī) means to support, to sustain; to withdraw, to pay; a branch (of a bank). It is written in 4 strokes, with the radical 支 (to support), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #79 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced zhī.

  • to support
  • to sustain
  • to withdraw
  • to pay
  • a branch (of a bank)

Decomposition

支 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Top + Bottom. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

支 =
十 top ten
又 bottom and

Radical

The radical of 支 is 支 — meaning to support. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

How the character is built

支 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A hand 又 holding a branch 十 for support.

HSK 4 Words Containing 支

Nǐ kěyǐ yòng Wēixìn zhīfù.
You can pay with WeChat Pay.
