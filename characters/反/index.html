How to write 反 (fǎn) — Stroke Order, Radical & Practice

反
fǎn reverse, opposite; contrary, anti- 4 strokes · Radical 又 (and) · HSK 4 required writing character
Quick Answer

反 (fǎn) means reverse, opposite; contrary, anti-. It is written in 4 strokes, with the radical 又 (and), and is one of the 150 HSK 4 required writing characters (rank #80 by appearance in HSK 4 vocabulary).

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced fǎn.

  • reverse
  • opposite
  • contrary
  • anti-

Decomposition

反 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

反 = 厂 outer cliff
又 inner and

Radical

The radical of 反 is 又 — meaning and. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 及取.

How the character is built

反 is an ideographic compound — its meaning is suggested by the combination of its parts rather than by sound. A hand 又 held up against a cliff 厂.

HSK 4 Words Containing 反

反对 fǎnduì to oppose; to be against
很多人反对这个计划。
Hěn duō rén fǎnduì zhège jìhuà.
Many people oppose this plan.
相反 xiāngfǎn opposite; contrary; on the contrary
结果跟我想的相反。
Jiéguǒ gēn wǒ xiǎng de xiāngfǎn.
The result was the opposite of what I expected.
