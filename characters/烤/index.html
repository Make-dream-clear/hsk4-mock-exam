烤 (kǎo) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

烤
kǎo to bake, to cook, to roast, to toast 10 strokes · Radical 火 (fire) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 烤 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

烤 (kǎo) means to bake, to cook, to roast, to toast. It is written in 10 strokes, with the radical 火 (fire), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced kǎo.

  • to bake
  • to cook
  • to roast
  • to toast

Decomposition

烤 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

烤 =
火 left fire
考 right to test

Radical

The radical of 烤 is 火 — meaning fire. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 烦烟.

How the character is built

烤 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 火 (fire) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 考 originally indicated the sound.
