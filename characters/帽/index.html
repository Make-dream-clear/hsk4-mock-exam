帽 (mào) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

帽
mào hat, cap 12 strokes · Radical 巾 (cloth) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 帽 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

帽 (mào) means hat, cap. It is written in 12 strokes, with the radical 巾 (cloth), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced mào.

  • hat
  • cap

Decomposition

帽 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

帽 = 巾 left cloth
冒 right to risk

Radical

The radical of 帽 is 巾 — meaning cloth. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 幅巾帅.

How the character is built

帽 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 巾 (towel) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 冒 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 帽
