操 (cāo) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

操
cāo to conduct, to manage, to run 16 strokes · Radical 扌 (hand) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 操 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

操 (cāo) means to conduct, to manage, to run. It is written in 16 strokes, with the radical 扌 (hand), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced cāo.

  • to conduct
  • to manage
  • to run

Decomposition

操 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Left + Right. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

操 =
扌 left hand
喿 right the sound of birds chirping

Radical

The radical of 操 is 扌 — meaning hand. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 抱技扔掉拉指挂推排按播擦.

How the character is built

操 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 扌 (hand) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 喿 originally indicated the sound.
