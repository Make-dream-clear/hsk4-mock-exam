厌 (yàn) — HSK 4 Recognition Character: Meaning, Radical & Stroke Order

厌
yàn to dislike, to detest; to reject; to satiate 6 strokes · Radical 厂 (cliff) · HSK 4 recognition character (认读字)
👁 Recognition only: the official HSK 4 syllabus asks you to recognize 厌 when reading — handwriting it is not required (that applies to the 150 writing characters). The stroke animation below is optional but helps memory.
Quick Answer

厌 (yàn) means to dislike, to detest; to reject; to satiate. It is written in 6 strokes, with the radical 厂 (cliff), and is one of the 441 HSK 4 recognition characters (认读字) — you must recognize it when reading, though handwriting it is not required.

Pronunciation & meaning

Pronounced yàn.

  • to dislike
  • to detest
  • to reject
  • to satiate

Decomposition

厌 breaks down into 2 components arranged as Upper-left envelops Inner. Recognizing the components makes the character easier to remember and write.

厌 = 厂 outer cliff
犬 inner dog

Radical

The radical of 厌 is 厂 — meaning cliff. Radicals are the indexing components used in Chinese dictionaries; they often hint at a character's broad meaning category.

Other HSK 4 characters that share this radical: 压厂原厕厨厚厉厅质.

How the character is built

厌 is a phono-semantic compound. The semantic component 厂 (cliff) carries the meaning, while the phonetic component 犬 originally indicated the sound.

HSK 4 Words Containing 厌

讨厌 tǎoyàn to dislike; annoying; hateful
我最讨厌下雨天。
Wǒ zuì tǎoyàn xià yǔ tiān.
I hate rainy days the most.
