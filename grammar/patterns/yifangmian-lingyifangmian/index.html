HSK 4 Pattern: 一方面…另一方面…

Understand the difference / 看区别3 fill exercises / 练填空3-question check / 测一测

Presents two relevant aspects, reasons, or considerations. They may support the same conclusion or pull in different directions.

一方面 + aspect A, 另一方面 + aspect B

Examples / 例句

一方面他想去留学，另一方面他又舍不得离开家人。
Yì fāngmiàn tā xiǎng qù liúxué, lìng yì fāngmiàn tā yòu shěbude líkāi jiārén.
On one hand he wants to study abroad; on the other, he can't bear to leave his family.
Two conflicting feelings of equal weight
这份工作一方面工资高，另一方面压力也大。
Zhè fèn gōngzuò yì fāngmiàn gōngzī gāo, lìng yì fāngmiàn yālì yě dà.
This job has a high salary on one hand, but also high pressure on the other.
Balancing pros and cons
一方面要保护环境，另一方面也要发展经济。
Yì fāngmiàn yào bǎohù huánjìng, lìng yì fāngmiàn yě yào fāzhǎn jīngjì.
On one hand we must protect the environment; on the other, develop the economy.
Two equally important goals
锻炼身体一方面能让人更健康，另一方面也能减轻压力。
Duànliàn shēntǐ yì fāngmiàn néng ràng rén gèng jiànkāng, lìng yì fāngmiàn yě néng jiǎnqīng yālì.
Exercising can on one hand make you healthier, and on the other relieve stress.
Two parallel benefits — frequent HSK 4 reading-passage structure
上网一方面给我们的生活带来了方便，另一方面也带来了一些新的问题。
Shàng wǎng yì fāngmiàn gěi wǒmen de shēnghuó dàilái le fāngbiàn, lìng yì fāngmiàn yě dàilái le yìxiē xīn de wèntí.
Going online has on one hand made our lives more convenient, and on the other brought some new problems.
Frames a balanced 利弊 (pros/cons) discussion — very HSK 4 essay-style

Choose the meaning first / 先看要表达什么

一方面下雨了，另一方面我没带伞，所以不想出门。Both facts support one decision. 一方面我想参加，另一方面又担心没时间。Here the considerations pull in different directions. “两方面”不要求一定相反，也不要求两件事毫无关联。

Common Errors & Meaning Differences / 易错点与表达区别

△ 一方面他想去，另一方面他又想去。
✓ 一方面他想去，另一方面他又担心费用太高。
For a useful two-aspect explanation, give the reader two distinct considerations. Repeating 他想去 adds no new aspect; this is a clarity problem rather than a ban on repeated words.
✓ 一方面下雨了，另一方面我没带伞，所以我不想出门。
✓ 一方面下雨了，另一方面我没带伞，所以我不想出门。
This sentence is acceptable: rain and having no umbrella are two reasons for staying inside. Rain does not cause someone to have forgotten an umbrella. 不要把两件有关联的事自动认作因果。
Easily confused: 一方面…另一方面 organizes two aspects. 不但…而且 adds a further point with emphasis. Both may fit the same facts; the context must specify the intended relationship. See comparison →

Fill every blank separately, using the meaning shown below the sentence. Spaces around words are ignored. Feedback lists reviewed answers; an unlisted rewrite is not automatically ungrammatical.

他想去留学，又舍不得家人。

On one hand study abroad, on the other miss family

Wanting to study abroad and being reluctant to leave family are two competing considerations.

一方面要保护环境，也要发展经济。

...on the other hand develop economy

另一方面 introduces the second aim alongside environmental protection.

这份工作工资高，另一方面压力也大。

On one hand high salary...

一方面 introduces high pay; 另一方面 adds the pressure of the same job.

Quick Quiz / 小测验

要把工作的两个优点并列介绍，不强调递进：___工作有意思，___工资也不低。
一方面…另一方面 matches the requested presentation of two aspects. 不但…而且 is grammatical too, but presents the second advantage as an added point. 此题选的是题干要求的表达方式，不是说另一句语法错。
要列出两个需要兼顾的方面：___要节约用水，___也不能影响正常生活。
Saving water and protecting normal life are two considerations to balance. 因为…所以 would make the second one a consequence of the first, rather than list two aims. 先找两个方面，再组织句子。
学中文___能了解中国文化，___对找工作也有帮助。
Two parallel benefits → 一方面…另一方面.

一方面…另一方面… FAQ

When is 一方面…另一方面… tested in HSK 4?

一方面…另一方面… (On one hand... on the other hand...) appears in HSK 4 reading comprehension (Q66-85) when texts use complex sentences, in 阅读排序 (Q56-65) where this pattern's connectors signal sentence order, and in listening 段落 (Q26-45) where the speaker uses it to express presents two aspects, reasons, or sides of a situation.

What's the most common mistake with 一方面…另一方面…?

一方面…另一方面 organizes two aspects. 不但…而且 adds a further point with emphasis. Both may fit the same facts; the context must specify the intended relationship.
