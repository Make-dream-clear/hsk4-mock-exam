HSK 4 词序 — Chinese Word Order

Arrange scrambled words into correct Chinese sentences and master where 把/被/比, adverbs, and connectors go. Word order is the core skill behind the HSK 4 看图造句 and 写短文 writing tasks.

2026 syllabus note: The standalone 排词成句 (word-ordering) task was removed from HSK 4 writing in the new HSK syllabus. But word order is now tested directly inside 看图造句 and 写短文 — so these drills are more useful than ever for building accurate sentences.
Word Order Essentials / 语序规则
1

Find the Time/Place Words

Time and place expressions usually come before the verb and often near the beginning of the sentence.

昨天晚上 我在图书馆 学了 两个小时。
2

Spot the Connectors

Paired connectors (虽然…但是, 不但…而且) tell you which clauses go together and in what order.

虽然很累，但是他还是坚持了。
3

Identify Topic & Subject

Chinese sentences often start with a topic or subject, but subjects can be omitted. Look for who/what the sentence is about.

这个问题，我们需要认真考虑。
4

Check 把/被/比 Position

These structural markers have fixed positions: 把 goes before the object, 被 before the agent, 比 between the two compared items.

他 把 作业 做完了。
作业 被 他 做完了。

    Word Order Quick Reference / 语序速查

    Choose a pattern that fits your meaning. Chinese word order varies with the construction; these examples are separate patterns, not slots to combine into one sentence. / 按意思选择句式，下面各行是独立例句，不要拼成一条固定顺序。

    PatternWhat to checkExample
    Time & placeTime can precede or follow the subject; 在 + place often precedes the action.昨天我在图书馆看书。
    我昨天在图书馆看书。
    把 + objectPut the affected object before the verb and describe the result.我已经把那本书看完了。
    ResultA result complement follows the verb; the object can follow the verb–result unit.我看完了那本书。
    Degree with 得With an object, repeating the verb is one useful pattern.他说中文说得很好。
    DurationKeep the duration with the action it measures.我看了两个小时的书。
    过 / 着These particles normally follow the verb, before its object.我看过这本书。
    他拿着一本书。

    This is a simplified overview — real Chinese sentences don't always use all positions, and some rules interact: (1) When 把 is used, the object moves before the verb. (2) When a verb has both an object and a degree complement (得…), one common solution is to repeat the verb: 他说中文说得很好. (3) 了 can appear right after the verb (completed action) or at the end of the sentence (changed state). For detailed rules, see our Grammar Guide.
