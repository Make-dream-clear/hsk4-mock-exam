HSK 4 把-Sentence Traps / 把字句陷阱

Practise object placement, commands with an implied subject, and 告诉 sentences. Use the examples to check word order and complements. Part of the 15 high-frequency HSK 4 traps collection.

语法练习

6. 把 + object + placement verb + 在 + place

To describe placing a specific object somewhere, practise 把 + object + 放/挂/贴 + 在 + place. Other constructions can describe a state: 书在桌子上; 我坐在椅子上 does not require 把.

✗ 我放书在桌子上。
✓ 我把书放在桌子上。
Place 书 after 把, before 放; 在桌子上 gives the destination. This rule concerns this placement construction, not every verb followed by 在.

语法练习

7. Commands can omit the understood subject

把这件衣服看一看 is a grammatical command with an implied 你. 请 adds politeness; it is not a subject. Keep the object after 把 and include a suitable verb phrase.

✗ 把看一看这道题。
✓ 把这道题看一看。
The subject 你 may be omitted. 看一看 completes the action phrase. 把看一看这道题 puts the verb in the object slot.

语法练习

8. 告诉 with 把: information before recipient

他把这件事告诉了我 and 他告诉了我这件事 are both grammatical. The 把 form puts the information before 告诉 and leaves the recipient after it. Do not swap their roles.

✗ 他把我告诉了这件事。
✓ 他把这件事告诉了我。
这件事 is the information; 我 is the recipient. 他告诉了我这件事 is also grammatical and conveys the same event.
